# Sector Reflection — Consumer Defensive — 2026-08-14

Diagnostic verdict: **TOOL/DATA failure** (pipeline total/band mismatch), with a secondary note that the sector reasoning itself had correctly landed on **up/mild** in the narrative but the official pipeline output flipped it to **up/notable**.

LESSON_BEGIN
ERROR_CATEGORY: B (TOOL/DATA failure — pipeline inconsistency)
TRIGGER_PATTERN: A sector call’s narrative and SECTOR_SCORES block cap magnitude at mild (component sum × multiplier = 4.0), but the deterministic pipeline prints a different `leading_sum`/`total_score` from the identical components and emits the official band as NOTABLE. The scoreboard grades the pipeline output rather than the narrative, converting a correct mild call into a magnitude miss.
CURRENT_BEHAVIOR: The official prediction is taken from the pipeline block even when it contradicts the explicitly computed component sum. Here, S0=1, S1=1, S2=0, S3=1, S4=1, multiplier=1.0 → 4.0 → up/mild in the narrative, but the pipeline printed `leading_sum: 5.0` and `total_score: 7.0` → up/notable. Scoreboard then records magnitude_hit=False against an actual up/mild day.
CORRECTED_BEHAVIOR: Before accepting the pipeline output, validate `leading_sum` and `total_score` against the SECTOR_SCORES components. If Σ(S0..S4) × multiplier equals 4.0 and the pipeline emits NOTABLE, treat the pipeline total as erroneous and emit up/mild until the pipeline logic is fixed. Additionally, on a follow-through session with flat futures and no fresh same-day scheduled catalyst, cap magnitude at mild unless a new catalyst or futures confirmation ≥0.5% is present.
EVIDENCE: 2026-08-14 XLP: components S0=1, S1=1, S2=0, S3=1, S4=1 with multiplier 1.0 ⇒ total 4.0. Narrative explicitly concluded “total +4.0 → up/mild.” Pipeline block listed identical components but produced `leading_sum=5.0` / `total_score=7.0` → up/notable. Actual XLP was +0.10%, SPY -0.20%, rel +0.30% → actual direction up/mild. If the narrative’s 4.0 had been emitted, magnitude would have been HIT. This is the same pattern flagged in the 2026-08-13 financial sector lesson candidate.

Secondary matches are the follow-through/outsized-reversal lessons (2026-08-14 communication services and 2026-08-13 technology), which say magnitude should generally be mild once the fresh catalyst is already embedded and futures are flat. No active Consumer Defensive lesson covered the pipeline-sum mismatch, so it did not prevent this error.
BACKWARD_CHECK: Applying the corrected behavior to 2026-08-14 would change the official output from up/notable to up/mild, making magnitude_hit True. It does not invalidate the active 08-13 consumer defensive lesson, because that lesson applied on the PPI-print day with a fresh same-day catalyst; 08-14 was the follow-through day with no fresh scheduled catalyst.
CONFLICT_CHECK: Potential conflict is with active 08-13 consumer defensive lesson (“allow notable when S3/S4 confirm after a second cool inflation print”). On 08-14 the PPI catalyst was already embedded, futures were flat, and there was no fresh scheduled macro release, so capping at mild does not violate it.
