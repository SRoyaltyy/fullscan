# Sector Reflection — Real Estate — 2026-08-12

Triage: **REASONING failure** — not a tool/data failure. The active 08-11 Real Estate lesson was applied, but it was one-sided: it treated the imminent CPI print as an automatic downside trigger for long-duration REITs, ignoring the visible 2Y easing-expectation tell. The cool CPI flipped the rate spine positive and XLRE outperformed.

LESSON_BEGIN
ERROR_CATEGORY: B
TRIGGER_PATTERN: A long-duration/rate-sensitive sector (REITs) has been lagging, real/nominal yields tick up into a scheduled CPI print, and premarket equity futures are positive. The model applies an active “CPI imminent => S0 negative for REITs” lesson without treating the CPI surprise as binary. It also sees a lower 2Y pre-CPI but treats it as a minor offset rather than an easing-expectation tell that a cool print could flip the rate spine and rally rate-sensitive assets.
CURRENT_BEHAVIOR: On CPI-imminent days for long-duration/rate-sensitive sectors, S0 is scored negative simply because the print is imminent and the 1m real-yield trend is elevated. S1/S2/S4 then compound the same negative rate spine, producing down/mild. The CPI is treated as one-sided downside risk rather than a two-sided binary catalyst.
CORRECTED_BEHAVIOR: When CPI is imminent for a long-duration/rate-sensitive sector, treat the catalyst as two-sided. Before scoring S0, check the pre-CPI yield-curve positioning: if the 2Y is drifting lower / easing expectations are visible, do not default S0 to -1; score at least 0 and consider positive S0, because a cool/in-line CPI would relieve the duration headwind and can make the sector rally. Reserve negative S0 for pre-CPI curves pricing hot/higher-for-longer outcomes (2Y rising, no easing tell) or for cases where the real-yield trend is strongly rising and no binary macro event is pending. Also avoid double-counting the same “rates rising” spine in both S0 and S1.
EVIDENCE: 2026-08-12 predicted XLRE down/mild (S0=-1, S1=-1, S2=-1, S4=-1, total -6.75). Actual: XLRE +0.93%, SPY +0.25%, rel +0.68%. Driver: July CPI printed cooler than expected, easing Fed rate pressure, pushing yields down and rallying rate-sensitive REITs. The morning analysis explicitly noted the 2Y was lower pre-CPI “suggesting some easing expectations,” but still scored S0 negative on the active 08-11 CPI-imminent lesson.
LESSON_MATCH_CHECK: Strong match with candidate 2026-08-12_sector_consumer_defensive_lesson, which describes the same bond-proxy/rate-sensitive + CPI-binary failure. It is also a direct refinement of active 2026-08-11_sector_real_estate_lesson, which caused the one-sided behavior. No tool/data issue and no pipeline-aggregation issue.
BACKWARD_CHECK: This correction would not have broken the 2026-08-10 and 2026-08-11 down-call hits. Those sessions were driven by genuinely negative rate/tape conditions, not by a pre-CPI two-sided setup with a visible 2Y easing tell. Applied to 2026-08-12, it would have moved S0 from -1 to at least 0 and likely produced an up or flat call, matching the actual cool-CPI relief rally.
CONFLICT_CHECK: Directly conflicts with active 2026-08-11_sector_real_estate_lesson; this new lesson supersedes/refines it — negative S0 is conditional on hot/binary downside, not automatic. It is consistent with candidate 2026-08-12_sector_consumer_defensive_lesson. No conflict with the technology/utilities/basic-materials/energy/industrials lessons, which concern different aggregation or relative-tape issues.
FALSIFIER: This corrected behavior would be wrong if the pre-CPI 2Y easing tell is present but CPI still prints hot, yields rise, and the rate-sensitive sector falls. It would also be falsified if CPI prints cool but REITs fall anyway due to an independent sector-specific shock (e.g., data-center capex cut, refinancing crisis). Therefore the lesson should be scoped to setups where the rate spine is the dominant driver.
DIVERGENCE_VERDICT: none_flagged
ACTIVE_LESSON_REVIEW: Active 2026-08-11_sector_real_estate_lesson was applied and is the proximate cause of the miss; it must be amended to make CPI binary and to weight the 2Y easing signal. Candidate 2026-08-12_sector_consumer_defensive_lesson should be accepted/merged into the shared “bond-proxy + CPI binary” lesson. Other recent candidates (tech, utilities, basic materials, comm services, consumer cyclical, energy, industrials) are not triggered by this sector outcome.
