CURRENT_BEHAVIOR: The 09-04 pending-macro asymmetry lesson is applied as a *directional mean shift* rather than a *conditional payoff shape*. CORRECTED_BEHAVIOR: (1) Treat the 09-04 asymmetry lesson as widening the *distribution*, not shifting the *mean*. (3) Apply a two-component version of the 09-10 triple-count rule: if S0 and S1 both trace to the same underlying macro object (risk-off/hawkish/oil), one of them must be zeroed. (4) Add a consistency gate: if the self-audit text concludes "flat," the component sum must reconcile to flat or the divergence must be explicitly justified in writing — a prose/sum disagreement is a bug in the sum, not a nuance.
