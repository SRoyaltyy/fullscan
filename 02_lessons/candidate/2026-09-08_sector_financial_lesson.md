# Sector Reflection — Financial — 2026-09-08

LESSON_BEGIN
ERROR_CATEGORY: B
TRIGGER_PATTERN: When a live geopolitical/oil supply-shock risk-off day is present (oil spiking toward $100, negative futures), and the sector is Financials (XLF), the model assumes a "value shield" — that financials will be relatively protected vs long-duration tech because they are value/cyclical. This assumption fails because financials carry credit/default sensitivity that tech does not. On a stagflation shock (oil → inflation → yields up → credit risk repricing), financials can underperform the broad market even when credit spreads are still tight (HY 2.65). The model must not treat "value" as a shield for financials on oil-driven stagflation days; instead, it must recognize that the oil → long-end yield → rate-sensitive financials channel is a direct negative transmission, not merely a broad risk-off overlay.
CURRENT_BEHAVIOR: On oil-driven risk-off days, the model scores S0 = −1 (not −2) for financials, reasoning that "financials are value/cyclical, relatively shielded vs long-duration tech" and that "credit is tight (HY 2.65), so this is not a hard financials risk-off." The model treats the oil spike as a broad equity tape negative counted once in S0, with the long-end yield stress (30Y 5.25%) scored as neutral in S1 (per the 08-17 lesson that long-end steepener ≠ NIM+). This results in a mild/flat magnitude call when the actual outcome is notable underperformance.
CORRECTED_BEHAVIOR: When a live oil spike (WTI +3% toward $94-100) is driven by geopolitical supply shock (Iran war), AND long-end yields are already in stress zone (30Y ≥ 5.2%), the model must recognize this as a stagflation shock to financials, not a neutral "value shield" day. The oil → inflation expectations → long-end yield → rate-sensitive financials channel is a direct negative for XLF (mortgage REITs, regionals with duration exposure, capital markets fee compression). Score S0 = −2 (not −1) when the oil spike is the dominant driver AND long-end yields are already elevated, because the transmission to financials is amplified by the rate-sensitivity channel. Score S1 = −0.5 (not 0) when long-end yields are in stress zone on an oil-driven inflation shock, because the bear/long-end steepener becomes a net negative for rate-sensitive financials even though it is not NIM+ (the 08-17 lesson prevents double-counting the steepener as positive, but the model must also recognize when the steepener is actively negative for financials via the rate-sensitivity channel). The "credit is tight" fact (HY 2.65) should temper but not eliminate the negative — the market prices forward credit deterioration on stagflation shocks before spreads actually blow out.
EVIDENCE: 2026-09-08 XLF actual: −1.38% vs SPY −0.55%, relative −0.83% (financials underperformed, not shielded). Morning prediction was down/flat (−2.925 total score) with S0 = −1, S1 = 0. The outcome review states: "The 'value shield' assumption failed to account for financials' credit sensitivity on a stagflation shock day." The transmission mechanism was: oil spike → inflation expectations → long-end yields elevated (30Y 5.25%) → rate-sensitive financials squeezed. Capital markets (MS/GS) and financial data (SPGI/CME) names were weak per MAP HEAT, confirming the rate-sensitivity channel. The gap-down open ($57.71) failed to find buyers, closing at $57.30 — sustained selling, not just an opening gap.
