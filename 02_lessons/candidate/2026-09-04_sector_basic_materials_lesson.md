CURRENT_BEHAVIOR: When the prior session's tape shows a decisive relative lag (1d rel −1.67%), the model treats it as live confirmation of continued sector downside (S4=−1) without checking whether that lag was driven by a T-1 catalyst whose transmission is already complete. CORRECTED_BEHAVIOR: Before scoring S4=−1 on prior-day tape, check whether the lag was driven by a T-1 catalyst (Fed decision, macro print, single-name shock) that has already had a full session to transmit. If the catalyst is T-1 and the current morning shows continuation-drift (not fresh shock), treat the prior-day tape as potentially exhausted and score S4=0 or require same-morning confirmation of continued downside. Additionally, when the same T-1 event appears in both S0 and S1, apply it once — if the gold slide is the S1 driver, do not also count it in S0 unless there is independent macro transmission (e.g., real yields still rising on the day, not just carryover).
