# Sector Reflection — Technology — 2026-08-14

LESSON_BEGIN
ERROR_CATEGORY: A
TRIGGER_PATTERN: A catalyst is labeled “fresh positive” based on deal size and participant names without checking how the market already traded it after the announcement. The catalyst is actually stale-negative (supplier financing its own customers = circular-financing alarm) and the relevant stock already fell days earlier on the same news. This stale-positive read is counted in S1, the multiplier, and the fresh-catalyst list, while a scheduled high-impact macro release is pending and the tech tape is crowded/extended.
CURRENT_BEHAVIOR: Treats large announced deals as unambiguously positive; does not inspect post-announcement price action; triple-counts a single catalyst; treats mild pre-market futures as confirmation; ignores scheduled-data risk; lets the deterministic pipeline emit NOTABLE even when the narrative cap is MILD.
CORRECTED_BEHAVIOR: Before scoring any catalyst, verify how the market already reacted to it. If the relevant stock/ETF fell on the news, classify it as stale-negative or neutral, not fresh-positive, and do not use it as a positive S1 driver, multiplier input, or fresh-catalyst support. Enumerate scheduled high-impact data at open; if one is due and tech is crowded/extended, treat futures as provisional, cap magnitude at MILD or lower, and explicitly include a flat/down scenario. Reconcile the narrative magnitude cap with the deterministic pipeline score so the pipeline cannot override a lesson-based cap.
EVIDENCE: XLK closed -0.40% vs SPY -0.20%; Broadcom -6% and AI/semis led declines; the Nvidia $500B financing deal was read as circular financing rather than a positive catalyst, and Nvidia had already fallen ~2.5% on Aug 10 on that exact news; July retail sales fell -0.6% and consumer sentiment dropped to 51.
LESSON_MATCH_CHECK: Matches the 2026-08-13 technology lesson (flat futures/follow-through → cap at MILD) and the 2026-08-14 communication services lesson (one catalyst double-counted into NOTABLE with soft-data/fade risk). It also aligns with the 2026-08-14 financial candidate’s flag that scheduled retail-sales risk was missing. No existing candidate captures the catalyst-sign/stale-negative circular-financing error specifically, so this diagnostic adds that trigger.
BACKWARD_CHECK: Yes. If the $500B financing had been scored as stale-negative/neutral after checking Nvidia’s prior reaction, S1 would not be +2, the multiplier would not be 1.1, and the official call would not be up/notable. If scheduled-data risk had been weighted, the call would have been flat/down or at worst flat, avoiding the direction miss and likely the magnitude miss.
APPLIED_LESSON_CHECK: The 08-10 stale-catalyst lesson should have fired but did not, because the model treated the catalyst as fresh via the active mega-cap-earnings-over-macro-drag lesson. That active lesson lacks a “market-confirmed positive” gate. The 08-13 cap-at-MILD lesson was written in the narrative, but the pipeline emitted NOTABLE from the same components, so the applied-lesson mechanism failed at the scoring/accounting layer.
CONFLICT_CHECK: No fundamental conflict. This refines mega-cap-earnings-over-macro-drag: do not forbid down when the mega-cap catalyst is not market-confirmed positive. It does not contradict the 2026-08-12 no-error lesson because that lesson presupposes fresh catalysts, benign macro, and positive futures — conditions that were not actually met here. It reinforces the 08-10 and 08-13 lessons.
FALSIFIER: The lesson would be weakened if the same known-negative/stale catalyst is followed by a broad up/notable tech day with no new positive catalyst, because requiring market confirmation would have caused an undercall. Also, if scheduled consumer-data prints routinely fail to hurt crowded tech, the scheduled-data downside component should be de-weighted.
DIVERGENCE_VERDICT: none_flagged — no leading-vs-tape divergence was flagged; the failure was catalyst misclassification, not a detected divergence, although weak NQ futures proved a better caution than the positive leading-factor stack.
ACTIVE_LESSON_REVIEW: mega-cap-earnings-over-macro-drag: keep active but add a gate — the catalyst must be market-confirmed positive. 08-10 stale-catalyst/crowding lesson: should have been applied here. 08-11 signed-component lesson: relevant; S4 should not be an independent magnitude booster. 08-12 no-error lesson: not relevant; conditions not met. 08-13 follow-through cap lesson: should be enforced in the deterministic pipeline, not just the narrative.
