# Sector Reflection — Healthcare — 2026-08-12

LESSON_BEGIN
ERROR_CATEGORY: B
TRIGGER_PATTERN: A large positive sector catalyst appears in the research summary and is dated in the HIT_GRID as today, but the underlying event actually happened in a prior week/month and the described constituent reaction (e.g., UNH/CVS/HUM surging 9-12%) is already in historical tape. The model treats a recap as a fresh same-day catalyst, especially after a strong 3d/1w relative run and when the trailing 1d relative return is flat.
CURRENT_BEHAVIOR: Scores the stale catalyst as a fresh S1 positive (e.g., S1 +2.5), keeps the multiplier constructive, and emits up/severe despite flat 1d relative tape. The narrative may even invoke the falsifier of the negative-catalyst lesson by claiming a “larger same-day positive catalyst” is present, when no same-day catalyst exists.
CORRECTED_BEHAVIOR: Before using a catalyst in S1, verify its source timestamp/event date. A catalyst is fresh only if it was announced after the prior close or this pre-market and is not already reflected in the trailing 1d tape/constituent moves. If the research note describes past price surges but the trailing 1d XLV relative return is flat, treat it as a recap, not a same-day factor. When the actual same-day driver is a broad macro event (e.g., benign CPI) that lifts XLV and SPY roughly equally, cap magnitude at up/mild or up/notable; severe requires a fresh, sector-specific catalyst plus confirming premarket/current-day relative strength.
