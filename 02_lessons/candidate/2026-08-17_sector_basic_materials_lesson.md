# Sector Reflection — Basic Materials — 2026-08-17

Primary triage: **reasoning failure** — the model let a fresh copper-specific bullish story override a broader same-day risk-off macro tape. A secondary tool/data issue (pipeline printing 13.2/severe vs narrative 6.6/notable) contaminated the magnitude grade, but that is already covered by existing candidate lessons.

LESSON_BEGIN
ERROR_CATEGORY: A
TRIGGER_PATTERN: A materials call has a strong commodity-specific bullish catalyst (fresh copper squeeze / record backwardation) while the same-morning macro tape is risk-off: US futures flat-to-mildly-negative, prior session SPY dragged lower by oil/stagflation, a China hard-data miss is released, and oil/geopolitical headlines are live. The model focuses on the sector-specific positive and misreads flat futures/Asia strength as bad-news-good confirmation, producing an up/severe call that is reversed by the macro drag.
CURRENT_BEHAVIOR: When copper is at record highs with a fresh supply squeeze and XLB 1d relative tape is positive, the model scores S1=2 and S4=1, applies a positive multiplier, and predicts up/severe even when broader equity conditions are fragile. It explicitly dismisses oil/geopolitical risk as “no headline” and treats Asia strength as confirmation, allowing the commodity story to override the macro tape.
CORRECTED_BEHAVIOR: Before weighting sector-specific positives, check the macro risk-off overlay. If US futures are flat-to-negative, the prior session saw SPY lower on oil/stagflation concerns, and a same-morning China hard-data miss is live, do not build an up/severe materials call on copper strength alone. Cap S1/S4 so the call is at most flat/defensive, require explicit acknowledgment of the macro override, and verify that the narrative SECTOR_SCORES total matches the deterministic pipeline total before grading.
EVIDENCE: 2026-08-17 XLB predicted up/severe; actual XLB -0.571%, SPY -0.473%, relative -0.098%. The model cited record copper backwardation and a fresh squeeze, but the market reversed on Middle East/oil risk-off and a China macro-miss backdrop (see candidate lesson 2026-08-17). The narrative SECTOR_SCORES block said up/notable 6.6 while the pipeline printed up/severe 13.2; the scoreboard graded the pipeline output, making the magnitude miss worse than the reasoning miss alone.
LESSON_MATCH_CHECK: Matches recent candidate lesson 2026-08-17 (same-morning China macro miss, stagflation/oil drag, flat futures, misread Asia strength). Also aligns with the active “temper severe” guardrail, which was checked but dismissed because the model wrongly saw no oil/geopolitical risk-off. The pipeline total-score mismatch matches existing 8/14 scoreboard-consistency candidates, so no new magnitude-only lesson is needed.
BACKWARD_CHECK: Corrected behavior would have avoided the 8/17 direction miss and is consistent with the 8/12 up/severe miss. It would not block the 8/13 down/mild hit, and it would not ban all materials up/severe calls; it only triggers when the macro tape is already risk-off. Conditional scope passes backward testing.
CONFLICT_CHECK: No conflict with active lessons. It reinforces the 8/14 guardrail (do not force all components negative/positive from tape alone) and the 8/12 extension (do not default to up on green futures). It extends “temper severe” to include oil/stagflation and China hard-data misses rather than only 1d rel <0.5%.
FALSIFIER: If this exact setup recurs — flat/weak US futures, prior SPY lower on oil/stagflation, same-day China hard-data miss, copper in record backwardation — and XLB still closes up with positive relative performance, then the lesson is too strong and should be narrowed to magnitude-only or discarded.
DIVERGENCE_VERDICT: none_flagged

The 2026-08-17 macro-miss lesson was not yet active/applied; promoting it for sector calls would have overridden the copper-positive S1=2/S4=1 construction.
