# Sector Reflection — Communication Services — 2026-09-04

LESSON_BEGIN
ERROR_CATEGORY: A
TRIGGER_PATTERN: When a scheduled high-impact macro release (NFP/CPI/FOMC) is pending and the sector is a long-duration growth book (XLC with META ~17% + Alphabet ~18-19%), the model treats "two-sided binary" as "symmetric risk" and scores S0=0, when the surrounding backdrop (hawkish Fed with 3 dissents for hike, Warsh hawkish, September hike odds ~54-57%, elevated real yields DFII10 2.45 +0.11 1w, 5-day 10Y-SPX correlation -0.943) makes the hot-print branch both more probable AND more damaging for this specific sector. The correct posture is a negative tilt (S0=-1 or flat-to-down), not neutral.
CURRENT_BEHAVIOR: Model identifies a same-day two-sided macro catalyst (NFP), observes green-but-unconfirmed futures (ES +0.09%, NQ +0.46%), and scores S0=0 with flat/flat prediction, treating the binary as symmetric despite: (a) hawkish Fed backdrop making hot-print more likely, (b) duration-heavy XLC book making hot-print more damaging than soft-print is helpful, (c) elevated and rising real yields already pressuring the sector, (d) -0.943 5-day 10Y-SPX correlation flagging the duration channel as live, (e) XLC already up +1.50% rel 1w / +1.82% rel 1m (mean-reversion risk elevated).
CORRECTED_BEHAVIOR: When a two-sided macro binary sits on top of a one-sided structural backdrop (hawkish Fed + elevated real yields + duration-sensitive sector), the model must score the asymmetric risk structure, not the symmetric binary. Specifically: if the Fed is already leaning hawkish (dissents, odds >50% for hike) and the sector is duration-heavy with real yields elevated/rising, then S0 should be -1 (not 0) because the hot-print branch is more probable AND more damaging. The flat call with 0.5 confidence should instead be flat-to-down or down/mild. The "two-sided" label applies to the macro print itself, not to the sector's risk exposure given the backdrop.

The outcome review confirmed: "When a two-sided binary sits on top of a hawkish Fed with elevated real yields and a -0.943 duration correlation, the correct pre-market posture for a duration-heavy sector is not neutral — it is negatively skewed."
