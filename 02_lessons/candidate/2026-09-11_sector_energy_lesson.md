LESSON_BEGIN
ERROR_CATEGORY: A
TRIGGER_PATTERN: A scheduled high-impact macro binary (CPI/NFP/FOMC) is pending at the open, US index futures are independently confirming a directional move of ≥ +0.5% across the major indices, and the sector's own commodity/underlying is offered — the model scores S0 = 0 (or negative) for a high-beta cyclical sector, treating the green equity tape as a "mild headwind" for a commodity sector rather than as a beta bid, and then applies the commodity-drag term to the ABSOLUTE print instead of the RELATIVE print.
CURRENT_BEHAVIOR: On 2026-09-11 the model wrote "the risk-on tape is a mild headwind for a commodity sector when the barrel is offered" and set S0 = 0, while setting S1 = −1 off the offered barrel. It then netted these into an absolute down/mild call. Reality: XLE +0.32% absolute (up), −0.53% relative (lag). The beta-lift term was positive and large; the barrel-drag term was negative and moderate; the correct model is XLE_absolute ≈ beta_lift + barrel_drag (net positive) and XLE_relative ≈ barrel_drag (negative). The model inverted the sign of the beta channel and applied the barrel term to the wrong quantity.
CORRECTED_BEHAVIOR: When a scheduled macro binary is pending and index futures are confirming ≥ +0.5% green, score S0 as a POSITIVE beta bid for high-beta cyclicals (energy, materials, financials, discretionary) — not as a headwind — regardless of the sector's own commodity sign. Express the sector-specific drag (offered barrel, weak commodity) as a RELATIVE-underperformance term, not as an absolute-down term. The default expression of "green tape + offered commodity" is up-absolute / lag-relative, not down-absolute. Only emit an absolute-down call when the sector-specific drag is large enough to overwhelm the beta lift (e.g., commodity collapse >5%, or a sector-idiosyncratic shock).
