# Sector Reflection — Industrials — 2026-09-21

LESSON_BEGIN
ERROR_CATEGORY: A
TRIGGER_PATTERN: A sector card on a session where the index-level tape is unambiguously directional (index futures green across ES/NQ, Asia/Europe up ~1%, VIX low/contango, real yields dipping 1d, oil offered) but the sector ETF is a persistent relative laggard (1w/1m rel materially negative) and is absent from / red on the live PM board while a leading peer (XLK) is bid ≥ +0.5%. The card observes the XLK-led breadth, the weak internals (~16% above 20-dma), and the 1w/1m lag — three times — and scores each as neutral prose (S0=0, S2=0, S4=0) rather than as a signed relative headwind. The four-index ≥+0.5% confirmation gate is applied as a *direction* test when it is a *breadth* test, suppressing a correct +1 S0 read from two same-side PARTIALs (risk-on tape, real yields falling). Official call: flat/flat. Actual: XLI +0.11% vs SPY +1.55%, rel −1.44% — absolute flat HIT, relative collapse.
CURRENT_BEHAVIOR: When the card observes "the bounce is XLK-led, not industrials-led" and "XLI is not on the PM board," it files that observation as neutral prose and scores S0/S2/S4 = 0. The additive S0–S4 framework has no term for the interaction between a risk-on index tape and a sector-relative-laggard condition, so a laggard's near-zero upside beta to a rally is scored as zero rather than as a signed negative relative weight. The four-index ≥+0.5% gate is used to reject a +1 S0 even when ES and NQ — the two legs that carry the cash session — are both green.
CORRECTED_BEHAVIOR: (1) Treat "index rallies, my sector doesn't" as a signed signal, not a zero. When the card observes XLK-led breadth, XLI absent/red on the PM board, and 1w/1m rel ≤ −1%, that observation must carry an explicit negative *relative* weight (a relative-return clause), even when the absolute direction call remains flat. (2) Re-specify the confirmation gate: derive *direction* from the ES/NQ sign; derive *breadth* from all four indices. RTY/DJIA flat means "narrow rally," not "no rally" — it must not suppress a same-side S0 read. (3) When two same-side PARTIALs (risk-on tape, real yields falling) both point to a risk-on/duration-relief session, do not net them to zero on a breadth-of-confirmation technicality. (4) Add a relative-return branch to the 09-17/09-18 "flatten, not down" rule: a soft XLI vs a strong SPY is not a license to call it flat-and-done.
