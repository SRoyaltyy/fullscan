## DIAGNOSTIC NARRATIVE — 2026-08-17

**TRIAGE.** This was a REASONING failure, not an upstream data/tool failure. All key evidence was retrieved and cited premarket: the China July hard-data miss (IP +4.5% vs 4.8%, retail +0.6%, unemployment 5.2%), the stagflation narrative that "pulled US stocks lower" Friday, Brent firm/Hormuz closed, and the Fed hike-odds collapse to ~25%. The components netted to UP because the carried Fed-easing repricing (B3 = +1, ×2 = +2.0 — the largest single positive, at LOW confidence) and an Asia rally read as bad-news-good confirmation outweighed the fresh negative cluster. That is misweighting, not missing evidence and not bad data. No KNOWABLE_AT_9AM shock is evident — the China data was out before the US open and Friday's stagflation selloff was known; no discount applied. **Category B.**

**CHECK 5 — Falsifier.** If this exact trigger recurs — fresh China/US hard-data growth miss + prior-session stagflation pull + carried Fed-easing hopes + no fresh index-relevant earnings catalyst — and SPX closes ≥ +0.5% (or flat-to-up) on 2 of the next 3 such days, the down-bias correction is wrong and must be narrowed or discarded.
