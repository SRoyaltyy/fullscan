# Sector Reflection — Communication Services — 2026-09-17

LESSON_BEGIN
ERROR_CATEGORY: A
TRIGGER_PATTERN: A mega-cap sector ETF is the *sole* non-participant on a broad risk-on premarket board (sector PM ≈ flat/0.00% while the growth/tech peer ETF is ≥ +1% and index futures are strongly green), on the session AFTER an already-printed high-impact macro event. The card correctly identifies the non-participation, then scores S2 = 0 and S4 = 0 because "XLK ≠ XLC" and "leftover RS is banned," converting an *isolated negative* observation into a *neutral* one.
CURRENT_BEHAVIOR: Treat flat-PM as a neutral observation and zero the breadth/tape channels; emit official flat/flat (or, via v2 tape_anchor + index_carry, up/mild) on the reasoning "no reason to be up ≠ reason to be down." The 09-16 lesson ("don't let NQ/ES create a bullish XLC call") is applied as a *symmetric* dampener that also discards sector-specific bearish evidence.
CORRECTED_BEHAVIOR: When a sector ETF is the *only* flat/negative print on an otherwise-green PM board and the funding source (chips/cyclicals/growth peer) is visibly bid, the correct prior is **down, not flat** — the marginal flow is leaving to fund the participants. Score S2 negative (not zero) when non-participation is *isolated* (one sector flat, all others green). The 09-16 lesson bans *bullish overlay creation* from index tape; it does NOT license *neutralizing* sector-specific bearish evidence. Apply the lesson asymmetrically: block the up-creation, keep the down-signal.
EVIDENCE: 2026-09-17 XLC PM +0.00% vs XLK +1.28% (only flat sector); SPY closed +1.134%, XLC −0.575%, rel −1.709%. Card wrote S2=0/S4=0 with explicit rationale "non-participation, not breadth expansion" and "XLK ≠ XLC." Outcome review: "The card identified the exact signal that predicted the day and then scored it zero." 0/4 directional factors correct. Same pattern recurs across the 09-17 candidate set (healthcare, consumer defensive, financials, industrials, real estate, utilities) — all-zero cards on a post-event risk-on rebound where the sector lags the growth peer.
LESSON_MATCH_CHECK: Matches the 09-16 Communication Services lesson (D-category tape_anchor overlay turned zeros into up via NQ/ES) — but that lesson's *scope* was "don't create bullish from index tape." This is a distinct, non-duplicate failure: the same card also zeroed the *bearish* sector-specific signal. Also matches the 09-17 sector_healthcare / consumer_defensive / financials candidates (net-non-positive card, growth ETF >> sector PM, v2 still writes up/mild). Not a duplicate — those are the up-creation side; this adds the down-suppression side.
BACKWARD_CHECK: Had S2 been scored −1 (isolated non-participation) and S4 held at 0, leading sum = −1, multiplier 0.9 → total ≈ −0.9, direction down/mild. Actual −0.575% / rel −1.71%. Direction HIT, magnitude HIT. The corrected behavior would have flipped a dir MISS into a dir HIT on the exact session. Backward test passes.
CONFLICT_CHECK: Conflicts with the standing rule "XLK ≠ XLC / do not map NQ/ES onto XLC" (08-27, 09-10, 09-16) and with "leftover RS is banned" (08-28). Resolution: those rules govern *mapping a peer's level onto the sector* and *reusing prior-close relative as a same-morning vote*. Neither governs the *information content of the live spread* between the sector's own PM print and the peer's PM print. The corrected behavior uses only live same-morning prints (XLC PM vs XLK PM), so it does not violate either rule. No unresolved conflict.
FALSIFIER: If on a future session a mega-cap sector ETF is the sole flat/negative print on a green PM board with the growth peer ≥ +1% bid, and the sector nonetheless closes *up or flat* (rel ≥ −0.3%), the "isolated non-participation ⇒ down" prior is falsified for that regime. Also falsified if the sector's own PM print is *green but lagging* (e.g. +0.3% vs peer +1.3%) rather than flat/negative — lagging-green is a different signal than isolated-flat.
DIVERGENCE_VERDICT: none_flagged

But it must be **narrowed**: it bans up-creation from index tape, not the use of the sector's own live PM spread as a bearish breadth signal. Recommend amending the 09-16 lesson with an explicit asymmetry clause and adding a new active lesson: "Isolated non-participation on a green PM board scores S2 negative, not zero."
