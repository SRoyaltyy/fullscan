LESSON_BEGIN
ERROR_CATEGORY: A
TRIGGER_PATTERN: When a scheduled high-impact macro release (NFP/CPI/FOMC) is pending and the model has already identified the pre-existing regime as hawkish (Fed/Warsh repricing, rising real yields, 10Y-SPX corr −0.943), the model treats the binary as symmetric and caps at flat/mild rather than pre-scoring the asymmetric downside for long-duration growth sectors. The model also fails to distinguish between the pipeline's deterministic flat output and its own narrative "up/mild" lean — the narrative and the scored output must be reconciled before submission.
CURRENT_BEHAVIOR: The model identifies NFP as a scheduled high-impact binary, notes the hawkish backdrop (real yields elevated, 5-day 10Y-SPX corr −0.943), but then scores S0=+0.5 on "green NQ + follow-through" while simultaneously capping magnitude at mild due to the binary. The total score (−0.45) maps to flat/flat via the pipeline, but the narrative text says "up/mild" — an unreconciled contradiction. The model also fails to pre-score the asymmetric downside: with a hot NFP print, long-duration tech (XLK) would face multiple compression on top of already-elevated real yields; a soft print merely relieves pressure. The asymmetry was knowable at the open.
CORRECTED_BEHAVIOR: When a scheduled high-impact macro release is pending AND the pre-existing regime is already hawkish (rising real yields, negative 10Y-SPX corr), the model must pre-score asymmetric downside for long-duration growth sectors: S0 should be negative or zero (not +0.5) because the upside from a soft print is capped (relief only) while the downside from a hot print is amplified (compounds existing duration stress). Additionally, the model must reconcile its narrative direction with the pipeline-computed total_score before submission — if the narrative says "up/mild" but the components sum to a flat call, the model must either adjust the components to match the narrative or adjust the narrative to match the components. A flat prediction into a scheduled binary with a known hawkish skew is a miss regardless of which way the binary resolves.
