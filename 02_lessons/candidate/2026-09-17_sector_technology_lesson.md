# Sector Reflection — Technology — 2026-09-17

LESSON_BEGIN
ERROR_CATEGORY: A
TRIGGER_PATTERN: Session AFTER an already-printed FOMC+SEP+Chair presser, with a large overnight ES/NQ-vs-prior-cash sleeve in tape_anchor (ES ≥ +1.5% / NQ ≥ +2%), oil offered, no fresh kinetic increment, no same-morning voter speech, and the sector ETF's own live premarket print green and leading the injected sector PM board (PM ≈ +1.3%, greenest of the set). The v2 engine nets S0–S4 to a small positive leading sum (S0=+1, S2=+1, S1=S3=S4=0) but the flat band discards the index sleeve + index_carry, emitting flat/flat while the sector closes +2.25%.
CURRENT_BEHAVIOR: When the sector's own live PM gap is green-but-modest and the sector's multi-horizon relative history is a laggard (3d/1w/1m rel negative), the model treats the PM gap as a *downside cap only* (09-14 "PM gap is direction, not a notable/severe extrapolant") and treats the leftover relative lag as forbidding up — so it caps the band at flat even though S0 (risk-on shared macro) and S2 (large-cap/high-beta leadership inside the sector) both scored positive and the index sleeve is unambiguously risk-on. The flat band then discards a total_score of 15.669 that the anchor + carry wrote.
CORRECTED_BEHAVIOR: On a post-event risk-on rebound where (a) the index sleeve is large and green (ES ≥ +1.5% / NQ ≥ +2%), (b) the sector ETF's own live PM print is green AND leads the injected sector PM board, and (c) S0 and S2 both score positive with no negative channel, the correct expression is **up/mild** (or up/flat), not flat/flat. The 09-14 rule ("PM gap is direction, not a notable/severe extrapolant") caps the *magnitude* at mild — it does not license discarding the direction. Leftover 3d/1w/1m relative lag is a *relative* note (S4=0), not a veto on an absolute up call when the live tape and the leading channels agree up. Do not let a laggard relative history convert a positive leading sum into flat.
EVIDENCE: Predicted flat/flat vs actual +2.2454% (dir MISS, mag MISS). The model's own Channel 2 text says "score and live PM agree up → keep direction; shrink confidence" and "Near-session call the components support: up / mild" — yet the pipeline emitted flat/flat. S0=+1 (risk_on, oil offered, VIX contango, NQ +2.1%), S2=+1 (XLK PM +1.28% leads every sector ETF; NQ leads ES; large-cap/high-beta leadership = the XLK thesis), S1=S3=S4=0. Leading sum +2 with no negative channel. The engine's own anchor (NQ +2.10%, ES +1.71%, PM:XLK +1.28%) and index_carry (1.846) wrote a large positive total (15.669) that the flat band threw away. This is the same failure mode as the 09-17 consumer-cyclical, basic-materials, communication-services, consumer-defensive, healthcare, industrials, real-estate, financial, and utilities candidate lessons — a cluster of nine same-session sector calls where the flat band discarded a positive index sleeve.
