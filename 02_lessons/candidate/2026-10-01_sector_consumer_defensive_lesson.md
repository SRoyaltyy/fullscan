# Sector Reflection — Consumer Defensive — 2026-10-01

LESSON_BEGIN
ERROR_CATEGORY: C
TRIGGER_PATTERN: A sector card whose leading factor sum is already signed (S1 or S0 nonzero, e.g. S1 = −0.5 on a cost leg) with the tape leg (green growth-led index), the PM leg (sector not a haven), and the cost leg all agreeing in sign — but where the card labels the card "unsigned" because the *net* is small, then invokes the 09-22 unsigned-card rule ("carry must not mint a sign") to flatten the signed sum to zero. The card has written the correct generalization (09-28: preserve a signed leading sum against disagreeing carry, both polarities) and then fails to apply it because it misclassifies a small-but-signed card as unsigned.
CURRENT_BEHAVIOR: On 2026-10-01 the Consumer Defensive card scored S0=0, S1=−0.5, S2=0, S3=0, S4=0 (leading sum −0.5, signed negative), then wrote "the card is unsigned" and applied the 09-22 mutable rule to emit flat/flat. The engine overrode with tape_anchor −0.976 and llm_overlay −1.2 to produce down/mild. Actual: XLP −0.335% vs SPY +0.178%, rel −0.513% — down/flat. Direction HIT, magnitude band MISS. The card's own prose contained the correct signed read ("mild, not dominant" rotation-out pressure; "if PM is not a haven, zero FTS credit" — which removes the only positive mechanism) and then discarded it.
CORRECTED_BEHAVIOR: When any of S0–S3 is nonzero and the tape/PM/cost legs agree in sign, the card is SIGNED, not unsigned — the 09-22 unsigned-card rule does not apply. Net the signed sum, then let carry adjust magnitude only, not sign (09-28 generalization, both polarities). A small signed sum (|leading| ≈ 0.5) maps to down/flat or up/flat, not flat/flat. "No positive license" (non-haven PM on a green tape) is not neutral — it is the absence of the only mechanism that could produce a positive print, which combined with mild rotation-out pressure is a small negative. Reserve flat/flat for cards where S0–S3 are genuinely all zero.
EVIDENCE: 2026-10-01 Consumer Defensive outcome: XLP −0.335% / SPY +0.178% / rel −0.513% (yfinance). Morning card S1 = −0.5 signed; card prose: "Risk-on / equity-beta expansion is [−] defensives... The rotation-out pressure is mild, not dominant" and "if PM is not a haven, zero FTS credit (09-15)" with PM XLP −0.26% vs XLK +0.58%. Card then wrote "the card is unsigned" and emitted flat/flat. Engine emitted down/mild (direction right, band one too aggressive). Truth = down/flat. Same error class as 09-28 (signed sum flattened by disagreeing carry) in opposite polarity — the 09-28 generalization was written and not applied.
