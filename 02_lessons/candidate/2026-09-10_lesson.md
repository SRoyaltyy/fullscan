The miss is a **magnitude over-call**: predicted `down/severe`, actual `down/mild` (SPX −0.58%). The morning file's own arithmetic (leading_sum −11.0, total −13.275, ×0.9) mapped to severe, and the narrative summary even restated "DOWN / MILD" while the pipeline emitted severe — an unreconciled band mismatch that the scoreboard graded against the pipeline. This is a **Category B (misweighted evidence)** error: the fresh kinetic/oil shock was correctly identified and correctly scored at B1=−3, but the *translation* of that shock into a severe index band was over-extended. The autopsy confirms the driver was knowable at 9AM (oil/Iran), so no 9AM-foreseeability discount applies — this was a reasoning/calibration failure, not a genuine shock.
