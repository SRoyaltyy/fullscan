# Sector Reflection — Real Estate — 2026-08-11

LESSON_BEGIN
ERROR_CATEGORY: C
TRIGGER_PATTERN: When an active geopolitical supply-shock/oil-risk-off story (e.g., Iran/Hormuz) is already building, a scheduled high-impact inflation print is imminent, and the target sector is long-duration/rate-sensitive (REITs), do not score S0 as 0 because premarket equity futures are flat and global equity indices are mildly positive. Treat the live macro risk-off as an asymmetric downside for REITs, and treat the 1m real-yield level as the operative duration horizon; 1d/1w real-yield easing is not a sufficient positive offset for a daily call when the 1m real-yield trend is still elevated.
CURRENT_BEHAVIOR: On 2026-08-11, the XLRE call scored S0=0 and S1=0, cited flat futures/mild global tape and easing 1d/1w real yields as offsets, and emitted down/flat. It underweighted the building Hormuz/Iran risk-off and pre-CPI caution; the hit grid even marked “real yields falling” as a HIT on the short-horizon easing despite the 1m real yield still elevated. The text leaned on strong REIT earnings/data-center positives as protection instead of treating the macro risk-off as dominant.
CORRECTED_BEHAVIOR: For a long-duration, rate-sensitive sector, when a live geopolitical/oil risk-off is in the news and a high-impact inflation print is scheduled, set S0_SHARED_MACRO to -1 (or at least negative) even if futures are only flat. Do not offset S0 with 1d/1w real-yield easing if DFII10/DGS10 1m changes are still positive/elevated; the 1m level governs the daily risk. Do not add S1 sector-fundamental positives as downside protection on a macro risk-off day. This should have produced down/mild instead of down/flat.
EVIDENCE: 2026-08-11 actual XLRE -0.72% vs SPY -0.32% (rel -0.40%); primary driver was shared macro risk-off — US-Iran/Hormuz standoff, oil ~$83, pre-CPI caution. Morning S0=0 was explicitly graded MISS (“underweighted risk-off”); S2 breadth and S4 tape were HITs; direction HIT, magnitude MISS.
LESSON_MATCH_CHECK: This is the same pattern as the 2026-08-11 candidate lessons for consumer cyclical, consumer defensive, healthcare, financials, industrials, and energy: a live geopolitical/oil + CPI risk-off was neutralized or offset by structural positives. No REIT-specific lesson existed in the scoreboard, so this adds the rate-sensitive/duration-specific extension.
BACKWARD_CHECK: Applying the rule to the only prior graded Real Estate run (2026-08-10, predicted down/mild, actual -1.29%, dir HIT, mag miss) would not flip the direction and would bias magnitude toward the correct risk-off side. It is consistent with the 2026-08-08 down/mild call. No prior REIT case is contradicted.
CONFLICT_CHECK: No conflict with the standing mega-cap-earnings-over-macro-drag lesson — REITs are not mega-cap earnings names and cannot override macro risk-off. It complements the rate-sensitive utilities lesson. It does not contradict “easing real yields are positive” when the easing is viewed over the 1m horizon or when no acute risk-off is active; the correction is specifically about horizon selection and geopolitical suppression.
FALSIFIER: The rule would be falsified if, with an active geopolitical/oil risk-off and pre-CPI caution present, XLRE closes positive or beats SPY while DFII10 1m is still rising/elevated. In that case the geopolitical-suppression bias would be too strong and S0 should remain 0 rather than -1.
DIVERGENCE_VERDICT: none_flagged
ACTIVE_LESSON_REVIEW: No active REIT-specific lesson existed. Relevant active/candidate lessons from the same date (consumer cyclical, consumer defensive, healthcare, financials, industrials, energy) all flag the live risk-off suppressor; this Real Estate outcome is another confirming instance. The general mega-cap earnings lesson and ops missing-predict-file lesson are not applicable.
