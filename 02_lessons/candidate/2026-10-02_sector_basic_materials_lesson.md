# Sector Reflection — Basic Materials — 2026-10-02

TRIGGER_PATTERN: A sector card whose leading factor sum is net-negative (S0–S3 ≤ −1) built from *stale level* descriptors (nested HEAT majority-down, prior-week ETF flows, multi-horizon relative-lag hole) on a session where (a) the live index tape is green pre-open (ES/NQ ≥ +0.5% or NQ leading), (b) the day's dominant driver is an *unprinted* scheduled macro binary (NFP/CPI/FOMC), and (c) the sector's own PM is a non-print — then the card treats the pre-binary real-yield *level* as a signed headwind while simultaneously declaring the binary unsigned, and resolves the divergence flag toward the stale factors instead of toward neutral.

Actual: XLB +0.70% / SPY +0.74% / rel −0.04% — a pure beta day, up/mild, zero sector alpha.
CORRECTED_BEHAVIOR: When the dominant driver is an unprinted scheduled macro binary and the pre-open index tape is green (ES/NQ ≥ +0.5%, NQ leading), do NOT emit a signed down call built from stale sector levels. (3) Collapse the triple-count: nested HEAT-down + prior-week flows + multi-horizon rel hole are ONE lagging fact, scored once, not three times. (4) When the divergence flag fires (leading sum negative vs S4 = 0) on an unsigned-binary day with green pre-open tape, resolve toward **flat-to-mildly-up with wide bands**, not toward the stale factors. (5) Do not apply ×1.25 session-direction weight to prior-week flow data on a macro-shock day.
