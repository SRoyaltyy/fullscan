# Sector Reflection — Financial — 2026-09-03

LESSON_BEGIN
ERROR_CATEGORY: B
TRIGGER_PATTERN: Sector ETF prediction where the prior session had a >+1% relative gain (XLF 1d rel +0.36% on 09-02 with BKX +1.58% / KRE +2.23% participation), the current morning has flat futures (ES +0.13% / NQ +0.16%), and the model scores all factors zero and predicts flat/flat — but the actual session delivers +1.56% absolute / +0.51% relative because the model applied the follow-through rule asymmetrically: it used the prior day's bounce to cap S4=0 ("already paid") but did not consider that a flat-futures morning after a broad participation bounce can still produce a strong absolute gain when the broad market rips (+1.05% SPY) on a macro catalyst (ISM Services beat) that the model explicitly identified as two-sided and refused to pre-score.
CURRENT_BEHAVIOR: When the prior session had a strong sector bounce (BKX +1.58%, KRE +2.23%) and the current morning has flat futures with no fresh catalyst, the model treats the bounce as fully paid and scores S4=0, S2=0, S1=0 → flat/flat. The model also refuses to pre-score same-day macro events (ISM Services) as two-sided, leaving no mechanism for a broad-market catalyst to lift the sector.
CORRECTED_BEHAVIOR: When a sector has a broad-participation bounce the prior day (not ETF-only carry) AND the morning has flat futures, the model must distinguish between (a) exhaustion setup (sector extended, commodity/rates headwind, 1w rel strongly positive) and (b) continuation setup (sector still lagging on 1w rel, no fresh sector-specific negative, macro catalyst pending). In case (b), a flat-futures morning after a participation bounce should NOT automatically cap S4=0 — the prior day's move can be the start of a rotation-in, not the end. Additionally, when a same-day macro catalyst (ISM Services) is identified as two-sided but the sector has positive sensitivity to that catalyst (financials benefit from strong services/ISM), the model should allow a modest positive tilt rather than forcing zero on all factors. The asymmetry error: the model used the prior day's bounce to cap magnitude at flat but did not consider that the same bounce could be the first leg of a multi-day rotation when the sector's 1w relative performance is still negative (−0.91%) and the macro catalyst is services-sector strength.
EVIDENCE: XLF actual +1.56% vs SPY +1.05% (rel +0.51%) on 2026-09-03. Prior day 09-02: XLF +0.80% / rel +0.36% with BKX +1.58% / KRE +2.23% participation. Morning tape: ES +0.13%, NQ +0.16%, premarket XLF +0.03%. Model scored S0=S1=S2=S3=S4=0 → flat/flat. The session saw ISM Services beat (~54.2+ actual) which lifted the broad market +1.05% and financials disproportionately (+0.51% rel). The model identified ISM as two-sided event risk but had no mechanism to translate a services-sector beat into financials outperformance. The 1w rel was still −0.91% (sector lagging), meaning the prior day's bounce was likely the START of rotation-in, not exhaustion.
