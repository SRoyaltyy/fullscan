# Sector Reflection — Industrials — 2026-10-01

TRIGGER_PATTERN: A sector card whose own governing lesson says "do not pre-score a pending spine print" (or equivalent: do not let a pending binary drive the direction score) — and the card then scores the sector-factor channel at a full −1 anyway, justified by secondary inputs the card itself describes as "mild," "already-printed condition," or "capped by rule." The pending print is nominally assigned to the magnitude band ("variance, not direction") while the direction score is still minted from the residual secondary factors. Companion symptom: a trailing relative-lag condition scored once in the breadth channel is then re-expressed as a directional `RELATIVE_LEAN` in the final call — a double-count that the card's own score-once rule forbids.
CURRENT_BEHAVIOR: On 2026-10-01 the Industrials card invoked the 09-25 lesson verbatim ("do not pre-score a pending spine print; the pre-open S1 is provisional") and then emitted S1 = −1, rationalizing it as "the BA halt + the already-printed core-capex condition + the capped grid backlog, not a pre-scored ISM miss." Netting a mild single-name negative plus a capped positive plus an already-paid condition to a full −1 while the actual session driver (ISM, 10:00 ET) was unprinted is a pre-score in disguise. The card then took the 1m relative lag — correctly scored once in S2 as a breadth condition — and re-emitted it as `RELATIVE_LEAN: underperform`, converting a condition into a directional bet. Result: predicted down/mild (engine) and flat/mild-with-underperform-lean (LLM) vs actual XLI +0.994% / SPY +0.178% / rel +0.816% — direction MISS, relative lean MISS in the most expensive direction.
CORRECTED_BEHAVIOR: When the sector's own spine print is pending at the open and is identified as the session, the sector-factor channel must be scored on the *pre-print-knowable* inputs only, and if those inputs are individually mild/capped/already-paid, the honest S1 is 0 — not −1. The pending print belongs to the magnitude band AND to a symmetric two-sided tail, not to a direction score. Separately: a trailing relative-lag condition may be scored once (breadth channel) and must NOT be re-expressed as a signed `RELATIVE_LEAN` unless there is an independent same-session relative fact (a fresh worse-than-index PM gap, a live rotation-out print, a confirmed flow). And when a sector is maximally oversold into a two-sided binary (RSI ~32, price ~5% below the 50-dma, uniform 4-horizon relative lag), the asymmetry of the payoff is knowable at the open: the miss is priced, the beat is not — so the honest expression is flat/mild with an *upside* tail, not flat/mild with a relative-short overlay.
