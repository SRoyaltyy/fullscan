# Sector Reflection — Consumer Cyclical — 2026-09-03

LESSON_BEGIN
ERROR_CATEGORY: B
TRIGGER_PATTERN: Sector ETF prediction where the model identifies a same-day macro catalyst (ISM Services) as "two-sided" and refuses to pre-score it, then the catalyst beats and the broad market rips (+1% SPY), while the sector call remains directionally down from stale consumer-weakness factors — the model treats "two-sided" as "unscoreable" rather than "symmetric risk around zero," and fails to recognize that a flat-futures morning with a pending high-impact macro print has asymmetric upside if the model's own S1 negatives are already stale/carried.
CURRENT_BEHAVIOR: Scores S0=0 on "two-sided" ISM Services (~54.2 consensus vs 54.1 prior) and carries stale consumer-weakness factors (July retail miss, Aug confidence collapse, TransUnion delinquencies) into S1=−1, then emits down/flat. The model's own research appendix notes ISM Services is "activity, not XLY spend" and treats the 10:00 print as unscoreable. When ISM beats, SPY +1.05% and XLY +1.39% (rel +0.35%), the down call misses both direction and magnitude.
CORRECTED_BEHAVIOR: When a high-impact macro print (ISM Services, NFP, CPI) is scheduled same-day and the model's S1 negatives are all stale/carried (no fresh same-morning consumer-weakness print), treat the pending print as a symmetric-risk event with a default tilt toward the sector's beta: if futures are flat and the model's own S1 negatives are >3 days old, a beat on a consensus-level activity print should cap the down call at flat (not down) and allow for a flat-to-up outcome. Specifically: (1) distinguish "two-sided" (symmetric, unscoreable) from "downside-skewed" (consensus below prior, would confirm weakness); (2) when the pending print is two-sided but the sector's negatives are stale, do not let stale S1 factors dominate a same-day catalyst that could resolve either way — the asymmetry is that a beat removes the stale-weakness narrative while a miss only confirms what is already priced; (3) if the model refuses to pre-score a two-sided print, it must also refuse to let stale S1 factors drive a directional call — the honest output is flat/flat with a note that the print resolves the day.
