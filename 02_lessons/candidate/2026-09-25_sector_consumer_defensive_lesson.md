# Sector Reflection — Consumer Defensive — 2026-09-25

LESSON_BEGIN
ERROR_CATEGORY: A
TRIGGER_PATTERN: A sector card whose load-bearing S0 input is a *rates* object (yield breaking a psychological level, deeply negative yield/SPX correlation, hawkish Fed-path language) while the live equity tape is GREEN (ES/NQ positive, Europe green) and the sector's own premarket print is a non-haven (mid/bottom of a green book, |PM| < ~0.2%). The card correctly refuses to mark the FTS spine MISS on a non-print, but then over-extends that correction into scoring the spine POSITIVE (+1) and publishing a "mildly positive vs SPY" relative lean — converting "unsigned" into "positive."
CURRENT_BEHAVIOR: On 2026-09-25 the Consumer Defensive card scored S0 = +1.0 with the explicit reasoning that the 09-23 correction ("don't mark the spine MISS on a non-print") justified a mildly positive S0, and published a relative lean of "mildly positive vs SPY (the rates bid)." The card's own text named the correct action — "at minimum leave the FTS spine unsigned rather than MISS" — and then did something stronger. It also noted in the same card that ES +0.28% / NQ +0.57% / Europe +0.64% meant "this is not a broad risk-off tape," and that PM:XLP −0.12% was "not a haven print," but did not let either fact reduce S0 below +1. Result: XLP +0.4406% vs SPY +0.5435% = rel −0.1029%. Absolute direction/magnitude HIT (up/mild, via beta), relative lean MISS.
CORRECTED_BEHAVIOR: When the rates object is the load-bearing S0 input, the *relative* leg requires a risk-off equity tape to fire. A yield back-up with green futures and green Europe is a growth-tolerant rates move — it lifts beta, not defensive relative performance. In that configuration: (a) score S0 at most +0.5 (absolute up via beta), (b) leave the relative lean UNSIGNED rather than positive, and (c) treat the card's own divergence flag plus a non-haven PM print as the tiebreaker AGAINST the relative leg, not as noise to be overridden. The distinction to encode: a non-print means "unsigned," not "positive." The 09-23 correction is a *discipline* rule (don't zero the spine on absence of evidence), not a *direction* rule (don't sign the spine positive on absence of evidence).
EVIDENCE: Morning card: S0 = +1.0; relative lean "mildly positive vs SPY (the rates bid)"; card text simultaneously states "this is not a broad risk-off tape" (ES +0.28%, NQ +0.57%, Europe +0.64%) and "XLP PM is −0.12%... That is not a haven print." Outcome: XLP +0.4406%, SPY +0.5435%, rel −0.1029%. Post-session review: "the rates configuration did not produce a relative defensive bid"; "XLP's +44 bp was a beta participation print, not a defensive bid"; "The correct S0 score today was +0.5 (absolute up via beta, relative unsigned), not +1.0." Corroborating: 09-23's FTS bid fired on a risk-off tape (XLP +1.34% rel); 09-25's did not fire on a risk-on tape (rel −0.10%). Same rates object, opposite tape, opposite relative outcome.

The 2026-09-25_sector_communication_services and _consumer_cyclical lessons cover cards whose own prose contradicts a negative sum. None covers a card whose own prose (green tape, non-haven PM) contradicts a POSITIVE S0 and a positive relative lean. This is a new, distinct trigger.
