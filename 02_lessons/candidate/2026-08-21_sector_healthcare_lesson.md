# Sector Reflection — Healthcare — 2026-08-21

LESSON_BEGIN
ERROR_CATEGORY: A
TRIGGER_PATTERN: A large positive trial/regulatory catalyst originates in one sector name one to two sessions earlier, but the prior/current tape shows broad sector-wide spillover — large-cap partner co-movement, sub-complex group move, multiple unrelated names participating, and a multi-week milestone/best-week context. The model nevertheless classifies the catalyst as “single-ticker,” applies the single-ticker no-domination rule, and uses a flat 1d relative tape to cap the magnitude at mild.
CURRENT_BEHAVIOR: When a major Phase 3/trial catalyst is carried into the session, the model immediately treats it as a single-ticker event because it originated with one company. It then uses a flat 1d relative print as a magnitude cap even when other evidence shows broad follow-through — partner moves, vaccine-complex breadth, sector rotation, and “best week since” language. This suppresses S1 and forces the magnitude band to mild.
CORRECTED_BEHAVIOR: Before applying the single-ticker breadth rule, audit the prior 1–2 sessions for catalyst spillover: did the large-cap partner/competitor move, did the sub-complex trade as a group, are multiple unrelated names up, and is the sector at a notable multi-week milestone? If spillover breadth is present, score the catalyst as a sector factor rather than a single-ticker qualifier and allow at least notable magnitude. Do not let a flat 1d relative print alone override a carried catalyst that has already re-rated the whole sub-sector, especially when SPY is positive and medium-term relative leadership is strong. The 08-13 reversal lesson applies only to a tech-led risk-on incoming tape; it is not a general excuse to prefer mild on a defensive/catalyst-driven tape.
EVIDENCE: 2026-08-21 Healthcare: predicted up/mild, actual XLV +1.29% vs SPY +0.41%, rel +0.88% — a notable miss in magnitude. The Moderna/Merck mRNA cancer vaccine Phase 3 catalyst was carried into the day with broad sector-wide spillover: Merck/vaccine complex up, Moderna +11.52% intraday after the prior pullback, HCA +5.6–6.0% on strong results, and healthcare headed for its best week since June 26. The morning framing of “single-ticker, not broad enough to drive the sector Net alone” was falsified by the actual breadth.
LESSON_MATCH_CHECK: Matches the scored error: direction hit but magnitude miss. The root cause is a reasoning error — misclassifying a sector-wide catalyst as single-name and letting a flat 1d tape cap the magnitude. No existing Healthcare lesson covers this broad-spillover exception, so a new lesson is warranted.
BACKWARD_CHECK: On the last 8 Healthcare scoreboard runs, only 2026-08-21 has this trigger pattern. The 08-14 and 08-17 down calls lacked the carried broad positive catalyst and would be unchanged. The 08-10, 08-12, and 08-18 up/severe calls are not capped by this lesson. The 08-11 and 08-13 directional misses lacked the required positive catalyst/spillover setup. No known backward regression.
CONFLICT_CHECK: No direct conflict. The 08-13 reversal lesson requires a tech-led risk-on tape; this lesson explicitly applies when that premise is absent. The 08-18 magnitude cap applies when SPY is down <1% on a risk-off day; here SPY was positive (+0.41%), so it does not trigger. The 08-14 policy-audit lesson remains valid and can coexist.
FALSIFIER: If a future Healthcare day has all trigger conditions — carried broad positive catalyst, positive SPY, strong 3d/1w leadership — but XLV closes with rel ≤ +0.3% or a mild band, then the broad-spillover magnitude upgrade is falsified. Conversely, repeated overcalls from this rule would justify requiring an even stronger multi-name breadth threshold.
