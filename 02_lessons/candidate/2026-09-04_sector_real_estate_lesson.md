# Sector Reflection — Real Estate — 2026-09-04

LESSON_BEGIN
ERROR_CATEGORY: B
TRIGGER_PATTERN: When a sector ETF is a rate-sensitive bond-proxy (REITs) with a persistent hawkish backdrop (30Y ~5.27% stress zone, real yields up 1w/1m, Fed held with 3 dissents for hike, Warsh signaling September hike risk), and the live curve is flat-to-slightly-down at the open following a prior-session relief rally, the model treats the flat open as a genuine offset and predicts flat — but the structural backdrop creates a knowable risk asymmetry: the flat open is more likely to resolve upward (rates grinding higher intraday) than downward, because the hawkish Fed repricing is the persistent dominant object and the relief rally is a one-day artifact. The model must not let a single morning snapshot of a flat-to-easing curve override the structural backdrop when the backdrop is unresolved and the prior session was a relief bounce within a 1w/1m downtrend.
CURRENT_BEHAVIOR: Scores S0=0, S1=0, S2=0, S4=0 and predicts flat/flat when the live curve is flat-to-easing at the open, treating the hawkish backdrop as "already printed (T+2)" and the 1d relative cushion (+0.14%) as evidence against a down call — even when the backdrop is unresolved (Warsh September hike risk, NFP tomorrow) and the sector is a 1w/1m laggard (−1.19% / −2.54% relative).
CORRECTED_BEHAVIOR: When the structural backdrop is hawkish and unresolved (30Y in stress zone, real yields rising 1w/1m, Fed dissent for hike, Warsh signaling future hike risk), and the sector is a rate-sensitive bond-proxy with 1w/1m relative lags, the model must pre-score asymmetric downside risk (S0 = −0.5, S1 = −0.5) rather than treating the flat-to-easing open as a symmetric offset. The flat open is a fragile assumption that requires the curve to stay flat all day; the probability of rates grinding higher intraday is at least as high as staying flat given the unresolved hawkish structure. A prior-session relief rally within a downtrend is a setup for a failed test, not a cushion — the 1d relative positive is a single-day artifact that does not offset the 3d/1w/1m structural lags. The model should map this to a mild DOWN direction with reduced confidence, not flat.
EVIDENCE: 2026-09-04: Morning predicted flat/flat (total 0.0) with live curve flat-to-slightly-down (10Y note +0.03%, 30Y bond +0.12% price up). Actual: rates reversed higher intraday (10Y to 4.74%), XLRE −0.72% vs SPY −0.39% (rel −0.34%), direction down/mild. The hawkish backdrop (30Y ~5.27%, DFII10 +11bp 1w, Fed held with 3 dissents for hike, Warsh Sep hike risk) was correctly identified but under-weighted — the morning's flat call required the curve to stay flat all day, which was a fragile assumption given the unresolved structural backdrop. The 9/3 relief rally (+1.19% XLRE) was treated as a cushion rather than a setup for a failed test.
