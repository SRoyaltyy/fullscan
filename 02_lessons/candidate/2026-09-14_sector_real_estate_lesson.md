# Sector Reflection — Real Estate — 2026-09-14

LESSON_BEGIN
ERROR_CATEGORY: A
TRIGGER_PATTERN: A rate-sensitive bond-proxy sector (XLRE/XLU/XLP) enters a session with a live premarket sector bid (green, top-3 of eleven sectors) on a tech-led risk-off tape, while the freshest prior-close 1d relative print is sub-gate (|1d rel| < ~0.4%) and the multi-horizon relative lags (3d/1w/1m) are negative. The model treats the premarket print as the "live tape," scores it as the S1 positive, AND simultaneously uses it to neutralize the S0 macro negatives (oil spike, USD strength, VIX backwardation, pre-FOMC event risk), producing a net-flat composite and a flat/up-lean call — while the deterministic pipeline (which lacks the narrative bias) calls down/mild and is correct.
CURRENT_BEHAVIOR: The premarket quote is promoted to "live confirmation" in the prose (S4 narrative) while being scored 0 in the arithmetic (S4 = 0), and the same object is used a second time as the S0 offset that cancels oil/USD/VIX. The self-audit explicitly claims "rotation-into-REITs scored once (S1)" — the double-count is invisible to the audit because the second use is framed as an "offset," not a "score." Live negatives that were correctly identified in prose (AI-complex unwind hitting the EQIX/DLR data-center sleeve; oil spike as a duration/inflation impulse; 30Y at 5.37% as a loaded spring with no cushion) are then not deducted, because the premarket positive is allowed to dominate the sum.
CORRECTED_BEHAVIOR: (1) A premarket quote in a low-liquidity, rate-sensitive ETF is an UNCONFIRMED signal, not the tape. It may not set the sign of the call and may not be used as an S0 offset. S4 must be scored on the cash session or left genuinely unscored — it cannot be zeroed in the arithmetic while cited as positive confirmation in the prose. (2) An object scored in S1 may not also appear as an S0 offset; if a sector object is used to neutralize a macro negative, that is a double-count in the offsetting direction and must be caught by the self-audit. (3) When live negatives are identified in prose (oil spike, AI unwind hitting a sector sleeve, VIX backwardation, a stress-level long end), they must be scored as deductions, not merely acknowledged. (4) The 09-11 "no live negative input" test must be applied to the FULL live stack, not just the rate channel. (5) Oil and rates are the SAME duration channel for a bond proxy — an oil-driven inflation impulse that lifts term premium/real yields is a rate impulse; scoring oil as "second-order background" while scoring rates as "flat" double-discounts one shock to zero.
