TRIGGER_PATTERN: An Industrials/XLI call is set to severe-up on strong structural sector factors (ISM expansion, AI-power/grid backlog, defense budgets) plus positive tape/flow confirmations, while a live two-sided geopolitical macro headline is active (e.g., US-Iran/Hormuz). CURRENT_BEHAVIOR: The model treats the constructive side of an unresolved geopolitical headline as a confirmed macro positive, scores S0 +1, leaves the multiplier at 1.2, and deterministically emits up/severe even though the headline is two-sided and the broad-market tape is not confirming risk-on.
CORRECTED_BEHAVIOR: When an active geopolitical/macro headline is unresolved (deal not finalized, Hormuz/Iran status contradictory, oil headline still live), do not count S0 +1 on the hopeful side. Mark S0 neutral and cap the Industrials magnitude band at notable/mild, even if S1-S4 remain positive. Keep direction up only if the sector tape and structural factors are confirming; require broad risk-on confirmation before emitting severe, rather than assuming sector-specific winners can produce a severe absolute move on a risk-off tape.
