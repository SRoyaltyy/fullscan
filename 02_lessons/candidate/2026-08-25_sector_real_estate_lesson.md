# Sector Reflection — Real Estate — 2026-08-25

LESSON_BEGIN
ERROR_CATEGORY: B
TRIGGER_PATTERN: A rate-sensitive sector call uses a yield-change table labeled through the prior close and treats those 1d changes as the live open tape for the current session. When long-end yields are already at a multi-decade high, the model applies the live-rate and level-vs-change lessons to force a down spine without verifying whether the current open/premarket curve is actually falling for a second day. An oil slide is noted but dismissed under a geopolitical-oil “doesn’t fire” heuristic, even though the oil → inflation → yield → bond-proxy channel is exactly the live driver. Result: S0/S1 are inverted, the direction is forced down, and the flat/close-to-flat actual becomes a miss.
CURRENT_BEHAVIOR: The model cites the 08-17 live-rate check and 08-21 level-vs-change lessons, but executes them on stale prior-close deltas rather than a true live curve; it treats a 30Y level near a 19-year high as sufficient to call down even when the live change is actually falling; and it dismisses the oil slide as “geo/oil doesn’t fire” because the 08-11 lesson was framed around geopolitical supply shocks. The positive recent relative tape is used only as a magnitude cap, not as a signal to re-examine the rate spine.
CORRECTED_BEHAVIOR: Before scoring a duration sector, verify the actual open/premarket Treasury curve direction — 10Y, 30Y, and TIPS real yield — from a live source, not the prior close’s 1d column. If yields are falling for a second day on an oil-slide/easing-inflation mechanism, treat that as a positive or neutral rate spine for REITs rather than a negative one. Restrict the 08-11 oil lesson to geopolitical oil spikes; do not apply it to oil slides that ease inflation and lower yields. When a positive relative tape coexists with a falling yield curve, do not force down/mild; at minimum move to flat, keeping magnitude muted only for structural reasons such as a stretched risk premium or two-sided policy catalyst.
EVIDENCE: On 2026-08-25 the prediction was down/mild with S0=-1 and S1=-1, based on “real yields RISING +0.05 1d” and “30Y at 5.27% near 19-year high.” Actual Treasury yields fell for a second day as oil slid (CNBC/Bloomberg 2026-08-25); XLRE closed +0.066% flat with relative performance -0.25%. The outcome review explicitly says the morning’s rising-yield premise inverted intraday and that the 08-11 oil lesson was misapplied to an oil-slide mechanism that mattered through the yield channel. The scoreboard entry also shows predicted None/None (a separate tool/extraction inconsistency), but the substantive forecasting failure was the inverted rate spine.
LESSON_MATCH_CHECK: This matches the recently proposed candidate 2026-08-25_sector_real_estate_lesson.md: a rate-sensitive call uses prior-close yield deltas as the live open tape and fails to verify the actual open/premarket curve. It also connects to active lessons 08-17 and 08-21, which were cited but not executed on genuine live data. No wholly new lesson is required; the existing candidate should be adopted and expanded with the oil-slide/easing-inflation distinction.
BACKWARD_CHECK: The corrected behavior fits prior sector history. It would have helped on 2026-08-17, where the model called up/notable but yields were rising and XLRE fell -0.97%. It would also have helped on 2026-08-21, where a high 30Y level plus an ambiguous change should have argued for flat rather than up/notable. It does not contradict 08-18, where down/mild was correct on a genuinely negative rate tape, nor 08-12’s two-sided-duration caution. No known prior graded run is made worse by verifying the live curve at the open.
CONFLICT_CHECK: No direct conflict with active lessons after refinement. The one tension is with the 08-11 “geo/oil doesn’t fire” lesson; that lesson must be narrowed to geopolitical oil supply spikes, not oil slides that lower inflation and yields. The corrected behavior also refines 08-17 live-rate check without invalidating it: the check must use a genuinely live open curve. It does not conflict with 08-18 relative-bid magnitude cap; once the direction spine is corrected, the relative bid can still cap magnitude.
FALSIFIER: A future session with a verified falling 10Y/30Y curve at the open, oil sliding, and no fresh sector-specific shock, in which XLRE still closes down more than -0.5%, would falsify the corrected behavior’s causal chain. Conversely, a session with verified rising yields and strongly positive XLRE tape would test whether the live rate spine should always dominate the tape.
DIVERGENCE_VERDICT: futures_right
ACTIVE_LESSON_REVIEW: 08-17 live-rate check — cited but not properly executed; hurt. 08-21 level-vs-change — over-weighted; the 30Y level alone forced down despite a falling live change; hurt. 08-18 relative bid magnitude cap — correctly capped magnitude, but did not repair direction; partial. 08-12 two-sided PCE/duration — correctly kept magnitude from being notable; mild help. 08-11 geo/oil doesn’t fire — misapplied; hurt. The net lesson is that “live” must mean actual open/premarket data, not the prior close’s 1d column.
