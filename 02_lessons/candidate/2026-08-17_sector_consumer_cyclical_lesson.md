# Sector Reflection — Consumer Cyclical — 2026-08-17

LESSON_BEGIN
ERROR_CATEGORY: D
TRIGGER_PATTERN: A sector call has a decisively negative fundamental spine from a prior-session hard-data macro miss (e.g., retail sales, consumer sentiment) plus an imminent sector-specific event risk (retailer earnings week), and the sector ETF already underperforms SPY across 3d/1w/1m. Premarket futures are flat (ES within ±0.5%) and there is no fresh same-day macro print. The model treats flat futures as a universal magnitude cap and downgrades the call from notable/severe to mild, producing a magnitude miss.
CURRENT_BEHAVIOR: Flat futures are used to cap magnitude at mild even when the direction call is supported by multiple independent fundamental spine hits, confirming relative tape, and an upcoming sector catalyst. In this case, the final narrative became down/mild and overrode the deterministic pipeline’s down/severe, with confidence cut despite leading factors and tape converging in the same direction.
CORRECTED_BEHAVIOR: The flat-futures cap should apply only when the call’s primary confirmation source is the premarket tape/broad-market follow-through. When the call is driven by a pre-existing hard-data spine and sector-specific event risk, flat futures should reduce confidence — not collapse magnitude. The final magnitude should stay at least notable when S1 is strongly negative (multiple hits), S2/S4 are negative, and there is no divergence. The pipeline-vs-narrative mismatch should be reconciled explicitly instead of letting the narrative silently downgrade the official band.
EVIDENCE: 2026-08-17 XLY closed -1.23% vs SPY -0.47% (rel -0.75), down through the session and near lows. The morning call was down/mild, but the pipeline printed down/severe; actual magnitude was notable. All six hit-grid items HIT, including retail sales miss (-0.6% vs +0.1%), UMich sentiment collapse (-8% to 51.0), rising credit delinquencies, ETF outflows, breadth failure, and rising real yields. Retailer earnings week (HD/LOW/TGT/WMT) was knowable at open. Flat futures did not prevent the sector’s decisive negative move.
LESSON_MATCH_CHECK: Yes. It matches the active premarket-tape-no-confirmation lesson, but that lesson was over-applied. It also matches the sector-specific consumer-cyclical-xly-is-called-down-mild-from-macro-caution lesson, whose DO-INSTEAD condition — “score sign conflicts with sector ETF tape / breadth” — was NOT met here because both the score and the tape were negative. The recent candidate lesson 2026-08-14_sector_energy_lesson (mechanical magnitude cap despite an active catalyst) and 2026-08-14_sector_financial_lesson / real_estate_lesson (narrative vs pipeline band mismatch) are also relevant.
BACKWARD_CHECK: If the corrected behavior had been in place, 2026-08-17 would have been called down/notable instead of down/mild. Direction already HIT; magnitude would also HIT against actual -1.23%. It does not contradict 2026-08-14, where the actual was -0.21% and lacked the earnings-week amplifier and the same level of convergent negative spine hits.
CONFLICT_CHECK: No fundamental conflict. The corrected behavior narrows the premarket-tape lesson rather than overturning it: flat futures still cap calls built solely on futures/tape confirmation, but they do not cap calls built on hard data and sector event risk. The consumer-cyclical “prefer flat/mild” DO-INSTEAD applies only when score sign conflicts with tape/breadth; here there was convergence, not conflict.
FALSIFIER: A future sector call with the same trigger conditions — hard-data macro miss, negative sector tape, scheduled sector-specific event, flat futures — that closes in the mild range (<0.5%) would falsify the “at least notable” part of the corrected rule. Also, if the relative tape is positive or S1 has only one weak hit, the corrected rule should not force notable.
DIVERGENCE_VERDICT: none_flagged
ACTIVE_LESSON_REVIEW: premarket-tape-no-confirmation was applied and caused the magnitude downgrade; it needs a carve-out for hard-data spine + sector-event risk. consumer-cyclical-xly-is-called-down-mild-from-macro-caution reinforced the mild bias; its DO-INSTEAD condition did not actually fire. The 08-14 energy lesson and 08-14 financial/real-estate pipeline-mismatch lessons are consistent and should be cross-linked for future sector calls.
