Result: flat/flat called on a day the sector rises >1% with the index up ~1.5%.

(3) When S2 reads "mega-cap bid, weak broad book," the correct output is "up but lagging SPY," not flat — breadth quality determines *relative*, not *absolute*, direction on a risk-on day. (4) Do not let carried/stale negatives (confidence, credit, nested MAP HEAT) fully offset *live* positives (retail sales +1.2%, claims 196k, SAAR 16.8M, real-yield relief) on a risk-on session. (5) When two futures feeds disagree, prefer the one consistent with the cross-asset confirmation set, not the one that happens to sit inside a magnitude gate.
