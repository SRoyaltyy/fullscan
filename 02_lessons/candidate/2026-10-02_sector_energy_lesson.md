# Sector Reflection — Energy — 2026-10-02

LESSON_BEGIN
ERROR_CATEGORY: A
TRIGGER_PATTERN: A sector card builds its entire signed directional case (S0 + S1 both negative) from a single fast-moving commodity's *morning snapshot* (crude offered ~3% pre-open) on a session where the sector ETF has already gapped to price that snapshot at the open, and the same crude-dip fact is scored with the same sign in two components (S0 rotation-out debit + S1 offered-barrel) — so when the commodity mean-reverts intraday, both components flip together and the emitted down/mild is a sign error.
CURRENT_BEHAVIOR: The card live-verified the morning barrel (Oilprice WTI $90.13 / Brent $100.07, CL=F −3.97%), treated it as the session's direction, debited S0 −0.5 on 09-21 laggard-on-green-tape rotation-out, and scored S1 −1 on "offered barrel + geo-premium fade" — explicitly citing the 10-01 trap ("do not sign down against a green barrel; today's barrel is red, so this is the analog of 09-25 HIT, not 10-01 MISS") while walking into that exact trap because the barrel was red only in the morning and recovered ~$1–2 into the close (WTI ~$91.11 / Brent ~$102.25). XLE opened −0.99% and closed +0.40% — a ~1.75% intraday reversal.
CORRECTED_BEHAVIOR: When the entire directional case rests on a single morning snapshot of a fast-moving, mean-reverting input (crude) AND the sector has already gapped to price it at the open, shrink the directional score toward zero rather than carrying the snapshot's sign into two components. Specifically: (a) do not score the same commodity-dip fact in both S0 and S1 with the same sign — that is a cross-component soft double-count that flips both together on reversal; (b) treat a pre-open commodity gap that the ETF has already priced as a coin flip, not a −1.5; (c) require a *fresh* intraday catalyst (not an overnight fade that has already printed) before signing the sector down on a commodity move; (d) when the sector is the premarket laggard on a green board but the laggard gap is *caused by* the same commodity snapshot, the 09-21 rotation-out debit is conditional on the commodity move *persisting* — if it is a snapshot, S0 should be 0 to +0.25, not −0.5.
EVIDENCE: Outcome: XLE +0.399% vs SPY +0.742%, rel −0.343% (flat-up, direction MISS). Morning live prints (Oilprice WTI $90.13 −2.95% / Brent $100.07 −2.19%) were intraday lows, not the close; WTI closed ~$91.11 / Brent ~$102.25 (World Oil Monitor / ConvexTrade). Card's own S0 guardrail said 09-21 "may debit S0 when the spine is not independently green — and this morning it is not" — but the spine turned green by the close, making the −0.5 a sign error. Card's own S1 text said "do not lift to −2 (09-22)" — correct — but the right answer was 0, not −1, because a morning dip that reverses is not a session direction. S2/S3/S4 zeros were correct and the 09-17 leftover-S4 gate worked.
LESSON_MATCH_CHECK: Matches candidate 2026-10-01_sector_energy_lesson.md in structure (laggard-on-green-tape + PM-as-sign sleeves stacked across S0/S2/S3/S4 to flip the oil spine to a down call) but with a critical difference: on 10-01 the oil spine was *green* and the card signed down against it (MISS); on 10-02 the oil spine was *red in the morning only* and the card signed down against a snapshot that reversed (MISS). Both are the same failure family — signing down on a laggard-on-green-tape read — but 10-02 adds the new element of a *transient* commodity snapshot driving two components. This is a refinement of the 10-01 energy lesson, not a duplicate.
BACKWARD_CHECK: Would this lesson have fixed 10-01? Partially — 10-01's error was signing down against a *green* barrel (the card even flagged it), which the existing 10-01 lesson already addresses. Would it have fixed 10-02? Yes — the corrected behavior (shrink toward zero when the directional case rests on a single morning commodity snapshot already priced by the gap; do not double-count the same fact in S0 and S1) would have moved the call from down/mild to flat/mild, converting a direction MISS into a HIT. Would it have broken 09-25 (down/mild, HIT, actual −0.89%)? No — 09-25's down call was not a single-snapshot commodity read; the backward test passes.
CONFLICT_CHECK: Conflicts with the 09-21 laggard-on-green-tape lesson as currently applied (which debits S0 when the sector is the premarket laggard on a green board). Resolution: 09-21 remains valid when the laggard gap is driven by a *persistent* sector-specific factor; it should NOT fire when the laggard gap is itself the priced-in snapshot of a mean-reverting commodity that has no fresh intraday catalyst. Also conflicts mildly with the 10-01 energy lesson's "keep direction, shrink confidence after mag misses" DO-INSTEAD — that guidance says keep direction; this lesson says the direction itself was the error when the spine is a transient snapshot. Resolution: "keep direction, shrink confidence" applies to *magnitude* misses on a valid directional read; it does not apply when the directional read is a snapshot artifact.
FALSIFIER: If on a future session a sector card signs down on a morning commodity snapshot that the ETF has already gapped to price, and the commodity *extends* lower through the session (no mean-reversion) with the sector closing down/mild, then this lesson is falsified — the snapshot read would have been correct and shrinking toward zero would have been the error.
