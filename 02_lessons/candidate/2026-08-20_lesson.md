All critical inputs were fetched and explicitly listed in the morning run: Walmart Q2 miss (comps +2.6% vs 3.8%, shares -6% premarket), Hormuz closed, Iran tanker attacks, Brent +2.91%, VIX 15.31 rising, EPU +82 1d, 30Y near 19-yr high. Actual SPX -0.87% (down/mild) means both axes missed.

The model did not retrieve or apply it.
