# Sector Reflection — Financial — 2026-09-21

LESSON_BEGIN
ERROR_CATEGORY: A
TRIGGER_PATTERN: A sector card whose leading components (S0–S3) are all zero while the index-level tape is unambiguously directional (index futures green, growth/tech leading by a wide margin, oil offered, yields easing) AND the sector's own premarket print is flat-to-red against a leading peer (PM:XLF ≤ 0 vs PM:XLK ≥ +0.5%). The card identifies the correct adverse factor (NQ/XLK leadership = inverse of rotation-into-banks) but nets it against a "ban on down" factor and collapses both to zero, producing flat/flat. The absolute call hits; the relative call misses by ~1.5% because the sector was the funding source for a growth-led melt-up.
CURRENT_BEHAVIOR: When two asymmetric factors are present — one that bans up (NQ/XLK lead) and one that bans down (modest green board) — the card treats them as symmetric and nets them to S0 = 0, then emits flat/flat. The card's own HIT_GRID listed "Sector rotation out of financials" as PARTIAL, but the score did not carry it. The 08-27 lesson (NQ/XLK lead = inverse of rotation-into-banks) was correctly cited but assigned zero weight instead of negative weight.
CORRECTED_BEHAVIOR: When a sector's own premarket print is ≤ 0 while a leading peer sector's PM is ≥ +0.5% AND index futures are green with growth/tech leading (NQ ≥ ES, oil offered, yields easing), do NOT net the two asymmetric factors to zero. Both point the same way for the sector's RELATIVE series: the index goes up without this sector, which is a relative-down configuration. Score S0 negative (not zero) for the lagging sector, and score S2 negative when 3d/1w/1m relative momentum is all red (persistent de-allocation is a live lid in risk-on tapes, not just "paid lag"). The absolute call may remain flat, but the card must not present a zero leading sum when the factor set is one-directionally adverse for the relative series.
EVIDENCE: 2026-09-21 XLF: PM +0.00% vs XLK +0.98%; NQ +0.41% leading ES +0.20%/RTY +0.08%/DJIA +0.11%; WTI −1.59%/Brent −1.02%/CL=F −5.94%; 10Y easing; XLF 3d/1w/1m rel −2.56/−2.34/−2.11%. Card scored S0=S1=S2=S3=S4=0, emitted flat/flat. Actual: XLF +0.036% vs SPY +1.552%, rel −1.516% — the largest one-day relative underperformance in the recent record, ~6–9× the trailing 1d rel the card treated as "at the sub-gate." XLF's high was the open; it was offered into a +1.55% tape all day. The card's own HIT_GRID had "Sector rotation out of financials" = PARTIAL and "Sector breadth failure" = MISS, i.e., the grid saw the signal the score zeroed.
LESSON_MATCH_CHECK: Matches the 2026-09-21_sector_consumer_defensive_lesson (net-negative leading card flattened by index_carry/sector_rs_veto in a risk-on regime) and the 2026-09-21_sector_basic_materials_lesson (persistently negative relative tape + red PM vs green futures flattened to flat/flat). It is the mirror image: those were negative-leading cards flattened to flat; this is a zero-leading card that should have been negative-leading. It also extends the 08-27 standing lesson (NQ/XLK lead = inverse of rotation-into-banks) by specifying the weight it should carry. Distinct from 09-18_sector_financial (all-zero card on a post-paid-FOMC session with modest mid-pack PM) because here the PM spread vs the leading peer was ~1% and the index tape was strongly directional — the asymmetry was knowable and large.
BACKWARD_CHECK: Applied to 09-17 and 09-18 (both flat/flat HITs on XLF −0.089% and −0.036%): on those sessions the index tape was NOT strongly directional (no NQ-led melt-up with oil offered and yields easing), so the asymmetric-adverse-factor condition would not have fired and the flat/flat calls would have been preserved. Applied to 09-16 (flat/flat MISS on −1.62%): the driver there was an unprinted FOMC binary, not a growth-led risk-on tape, so this lesson would not have changed that call. Applied to 09-11 (flat/flat MISS on +0.67%): that was an up-miss, not a relative-down miss; this lesson is silent on it. No prior HIT is converted to a MISS by this correction.
CONFLICT_CHECK: Conflicts with the 08-28 lesson ("trailing outflows/relative lag are not a 1-day lid") as currently written without a regime qualifier. Resolution: the 08-28 lesson holds in flat/range tapes; in a strongly directional risk-on tape (index futures green, growth leading, oil offered), persistent negative relative momentum is a live lid and should carry negative S2 weight. This is a regime qualifier, not a repeal. Also conflicts with the 08-21 lesson ("modest green board = ban on down, not a license for up") — resolution: the ban-on-down reading is correct for the ABSOLUTE call but must not be netted against the adverse relative factor to produce a zero leading sum; the two operate on different series (absolute vs relative).
FALSIFIER: If a future session presents the same configuration (sector PM ≤ 0 vs leading-peer PM ≥ +0.5%, NQ ≥ ES, oil offered, yields easing, 3d/1w/1m rel all red) and the sector instead closes with rel ≥ −0.3% (i.e., it participates in or outperforms the risk-on tape), then the asymmetric-adverse-factor rule is falsified and the zero-weight treatment was correct. A single such observation would downgrade the rule to "context only."
DIVERGENCE_VERDICT: none_flagged
