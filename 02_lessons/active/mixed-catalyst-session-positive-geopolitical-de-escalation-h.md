## RULE
No change. When leading indicators are offsetting and futures give no confirmation, hold the flat band at low confidence; weight a positive headline only when Europe/US futures confirm it (08-03 rule), and weight an Asia crash only when futures independently turn negative (07-31 rule).

## WHEN IT FIRES
Mixed-catalyst session — positive geopolitical de-escalation headline versus a single-market Asia chip crash, with US futures flat and giving no confirmation in either direction; low-conviction flat-band call is the correct calibration.

## WRONG IF
If this exact pattern recurs (positive de-escalation headline + Asia chip crash + flat futures) and SPX moves >=1.0% in either direction, the flat-band default is wrong and must be recalibrated. Also, if a futures-confirmed positive catalyst ever yields a down day, candidate 08-03 must be revised.
