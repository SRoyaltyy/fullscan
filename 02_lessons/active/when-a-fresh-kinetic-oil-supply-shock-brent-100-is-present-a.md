## RULE
When the 8/18 metals-co-move floor ban is firing (oil spiking into equity risk-off, copper/gold co-moving DOWN with equities), the model must recognize that this eliminates the only potential breadth offset within XLB. If all four S1 sub-factors align negative (chemicals cost drag + copper fade + gold fade + China contraction) with zero offsetting positive, score S1 = −2 regardless of whether copper is "near records" — the composition-weighted reality is that the majority sleeve (chemicals) is negative AND the minority sleeve (copper miners) that could have provided offset is also negative. Similarly, score S2 = −1 when the 8/18 pattern fires because breadth is uniformly negative — there is no defensive pocket (gold miners are also sliding). The "not a collapse" reasoning only justifies capping S1 when there is SOME offsetting positive; when all sub-channels align negative, the cap does not apply.

## WHEN IT FIRES
When a fresh kinetic/oil-supply shock (Brent >$100) is present at the open with a sector whose dominant sleeve (chemicals ~40-50% of XLB) faces a direct cost headwind, AND all four sector-factor sub-channels (chemicals cost drag, copper fade, gold fade, China contraction) align negative simultaneously with no offsetting positive anywhere in the book, the model scores S1 = −1 (capped below −2 on the reasoning that copper is "still near records, not a collapse") and S2 = 0 (calling it "not a clean breadth expansion or failure"). The model underweights magnitude when the composition-weighted reality shows zero offset — the majority sleeve is negative AND the minority sleeve that previously provided offset is also turning negative.

## WRONG IF
A future session where the 8/18 metals-co-move pattern fires (oil >$100 into equity risk-off), all four S1 sub-factors align negative, but XLB declines less than 0.5% (mild magnitude) despite S1 = −2 and S2 = −1 scoring. If the sector shows resilience despite the uniform negative alignment, the magnitude underweight correction would be disproven.

## EVIDENCE
2026-09-09: Predicted down/mild (total −1.8), actual XLB −1.06% (notable magnitude, rel −0.59%). S1 = −1 but all four negative factors fired (chemicals oil-cost drag HIT, copper fading HIT, China PMI contraction HIT, gold fade HIT) with zero offset. S2 = 0 but the sector experienced broad-based weakness — chemicals, metals, and gold miners all declined together (XLB down 1.06% vs SPY down 0.46%, more than 2x SPY's decline). The 8/18 metals-co-move pattern firing meant no defensive pocket existed within XLB. Outcome verdict: "S0/S1 were directionally right but under-scored; S2 should have been −1 not 0.
