## RULE
(1) Never map NQ/ES divergence onto XLC. XLC's core is META (~17%) + Alphabet A+C (~18–19%); NQ is a semis/software/AI-infra index. A weak NQ tells you nothing about whether the two anchors are being bought — and the morning note itself identified the premarket leaders as names *outside* the ETF. Retire the 09-08 "NQ/ES divergence is a direct signal about XLC's core" clause. (2) Do not score S1 = 0 by default for a two-name book. Absence of a *fresh catalyst* is not absence of *relative strength*; a concentrated book of mega-cap FCF machines is a natural risk-off rotation destination and can outperform with zero news. Require an explicit positive/negative S1 judgment rather than a default zero. (3) When the only non-zero terms (S0 macro, S2 breadth) are two expressions of the same oil-shock regime, do not read their agreement as confirmation — that is correlated double-counting, and the effective negative is smaller than the nominal sum. (4) The 09-04 asymmetric-downside-into-CPI tilt is a *macro* tilt (it correctly predicted SPY −0.60% / Nasdaq −0.70%); it says nothing about a sector's *relative* return and must not be allowed to drive the sector call.

## WHEN IT FIRES
A sector ETF is a concentrated two-name book (top-2 weights ≈ 35–40% of the fund) and the model infers the sector's direction from a broad-tech index-futures composition signal (NQ vs ES divergence) rather than from the specific holdings' own tape or flow; the broad-tech signal is then scored as an independent negative alongside a macro negative that expresses the same regime.

## WRONG IF
This lesson is falsified if, on a future session with (a) a concentrated two-name XLC book, (b) NQ lagging ES by ≥25bp, and (c) a live oil/hawkish-Fed macro drag, XLC nonetheless *underperforms* SPY — i.e. if the NQ/ES gap turns out to carry genuine information about META/Alphabet after all. A second falsifier: if a future two-name-book session with no fresh catalyst and no flow print produces XLC *underperformance*, then the "absence of catalyst ≠ absence of relative strength" clause is wrong and S1 = 0 by default is defensible. A third: if the correlated-negative double-count correction is wrong, then sessions where S0 and S2 are both negative on the same regime should reliably produce down outcomes for XLC — today they did not.
