## RULE
When futures are flat but the macro tape shows a clear directional tilt (yields falling meaningfully, rate-cut odds rising, risk-on currency dynamics), the model should at minimum score S0 as +1 (or at least flag the risk-on tilt as a live factor) rather than defaulting to 0 because futures are inside ±0.5%. The flat-futures test should be a veto on extreme calls (notable up/down), not a veto on mild directional calls when other macro channels confirm a tilt. Additionally, when a top-2 holding has a standing AI-monetization thesis (S1 spine is "AI monetization proof"), the model should recognize that any fresh AI-model/capability claim from META or GOOGL is a potential same-day catalyst — the spine thesis is not just "carried," it is a live catalyst family that can fire intraday. The model should also model two-name divergence: a +4% META with a −0.3% GOOGL still nets positive for XLC on a risk-on day; it should not require both leaders to confirm.

## WHEN IT FIRES
Sector prediction calls flat/flat when broad-market futures are flat (+0.13%) but the macro tape has a clear risk-on tilt (yields retreating, rate-cut hopes rising, yen dynamics supportive). The model treats "futures inside ±0.5%" as conclusive evidence of no directional day, ignoring that futures can be flat at open while the day's macro direction (risk-on/risk-off) is already inferable from yield moves, currency dynamics, and prior-session momentum. Additionally, the model has no mechanism to anticipate that a top-2 holding (META ~17%) could rip intraday on a fresh AI-model catalyst announced mid-session — but more importantly, it fails to distinguish "no fresh catalyst at open" from "no catalyst possible today," and it treats a two-name book (META+GOOGL ~35%) as requiring both leaders to move together, when divergence (META +4% vs GOOGL −0.3%) still produces a positive XLC day on a broad risk-on tape.

## WRONG IF
A day where futures are flat, yields are retreating on rate-cut hopes, but XLC closes flat or down despite SPY rising — if the model scores S0=+1 on the risk-on tilt and XLC still fails to participate (e.g., a META/GOOGL-specific negative catalyst offsets the macro tailwind), the lesson would be falsified. Also falsified if a future risk-on-tilt day with flat futures produces XLC down >0.5% with no offsetting sector-specific catalyst.

## EVIDENCE
2026-09-03: XLC +0.85% (mild up) vs predicted flat/flat. SPY +1.05% (best day in a month) on yields retreating/rate-cut hopes. META +4% intraday on Muse Spark 1.3 AI model claim (published ~2:48 PM ET, not knowable at open). GOOGL −0.3% on AI-talent concerns. The morning correctly identified no fresh premarket catalyst but missed that (a) the macro tape had a risk-on tilt despite flat futures, and (b) the standing AI-monetization spine could fire intraday via a Meta model announcement.
