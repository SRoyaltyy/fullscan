## RULE
When the structural backdrop is hawkish and unresolved (30Y in stress zone, real yields rising 1w/1m, Fed dissent for hike, Warsh signaling future hike risk), and the sector is a rate-sensitive bond-proxy with 1w/1m relative lags, the model must pre-score asymmetric downside risk (S0 = −0.5, S1 = −0.5) rather than treating the flat-to-easing open as a symmetric offset. The flat open is a fragile assumption that requires the curve to stay flat all day; the probability of rates grinding higher intraday is at least as high as staying flat given the unresolved hawkish structure. A prior-session relief rally within a downtrend is a setup for a failed test, not a cushion — the 1d relative positive is a single-day artifact that does not offset the 3d/1w/1m structural lags. The model should map this to a mild DOWN direction with reduced confidence, not flat.

## WHEN IT FIRES
When a sector ETF is a rate-sensitive bond-proxy (REITs) with a persistent hawkish backdrop (30Y ~5.27% stress zone, real yields up 1w/1m, Fed held with 3 dissents for hike, Warsh signaling September hike risk), and the live curve is flat-to-slightly-down at the open following a prior-session relief rally, the model treats the flat open as a genuine offset and predicts flat — but the structural backdrop creates a knowable risk asymmetry: the flat open is more likely to resolve upward (rates grinding higher intraday) than downward, because the hawkish Fed repricing is the persistent dominant object and the relief rally is a one-day artifact. The model must not let a single morning snapshot of a flat-to-easing curve override the structural backdrop when the backdrop is unresolved and the prior session was a relief bounce within a 1w/1m downtrend.

## WRONG IF
A session where the live curve is flat-to-easing at the open, the backdrop is hawkish and unresolved (30Y stress, Fed dissent for hike), but rates stay flat or fall intraday and the sector ETF holds flat or rises — if the model pre-scores asymmetric downside and is wrong on multiple such occasions, the lesson is overfit. Also falsified if a verified second-day falling curve (per 08-25) produces a down day despite the easing backdrop.

## EVIDENCE

Actual: rates reversed higher intraday (10Y to 4.74%), XLRE −0.72% vs SPY −0.39% (rel −0.34%), direction down/mild. The hawkish backdrop (30Y ~5.27%, DFII10 +11bp 1w, Fed held with 3 dissents for hike, Warsh Sep hike risk) was correctly identified but under-weighted — the morning's flat call required the curve to stay flat all day, which was a fragile assumption given the unresolved structural backdrop. The 9/3 relief rally (+1.19% XLRE) was treated as a cushion rather than a setup for a failed test.
