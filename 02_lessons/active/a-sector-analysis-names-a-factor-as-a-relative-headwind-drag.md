## RULE
If a factor is named in the prose as a "headwind," "drag," "rotation away from," or "relative negative," it MUST receive a negative score in the corresponding component — or the prose must be deleted. No unscored named factors. Concretely for a defensive sector on a green-futures / oil-offering / in-line-CPI tape: score the risk-on rotation as a NEGATIVE S1 (or S0) contribution of comparable magnitude to the input-cost-relief positive, so the two partially cancel rather than one being counted and the other merely narrated. Additionally, when the packet's own analysis identifies a risk-on-leaning tape, emit an explicit RELATIVE direction lean (negative for defensives) alongside the absolute direction — the current output format predicts only absolute direction/magnitude, which is why a −0.50% relative miss can coexist with a scored "HIT." Finally, do not use a narrow futures-spread dampener ("NQ leads ES by only 2bp") to downgrade a rotation that is corroborated by four independent same-morning signals (green ES/NQ, green Europe, oil offering, VIX backwardation resolving benignly on an in-line CPI).

## WHEN IT FIRES
A sector analysis names a factor as a "relative headwind" / "drag" / "rotation away from" in the prose of a component section, but assigns that component a NON-NEGATIVE score (0 or positive) because the same section also contains a scored positive factor (e.g., input-cost relief). The named negative is left unscored while the named positive is quantified, producing an upward-biased leading sum and a relative-direction miss on a day the absolute direction is correct.

## WRONG IF
This lesson is falsified if, on a future session where a defensive sector's packet names a risk-on rotation as a relative headwind but scores the component non-negative, the sector nonetheless closes with relative OUTPERFORMANCE (rel ≥ 0) — i.e., the named rotation fails to transmit. It is also falsified if scoring the rotation symmetrically produces a WORSE relative-direction call than leaving it unscored across a sample of ≥5 such sessions. A single counter-instance where the rotation is named, unscored, and the sector still outperforms relatively would weaken but not kill the trigger (the rotation may be offset by a stronger unmodeled bid); two or more would require narrowing the trigger to require the four-signal corroboration (green ES/NQ + green Europe + oil offering + benign vol resolution).
