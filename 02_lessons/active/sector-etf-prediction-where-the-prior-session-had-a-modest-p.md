## RULE
When a same-day macro catalyst (ISM Services, claims, CPI, NFP) is identified as two-sided but has a consensus expectation that, if beaten, would drive broad-market upside (SPY >+0.5%), the model must not encode the catalyst purely as "two-sided = no score." Instead, it should (a) recognize that a two-sided catalyst with a beat-consensus skew creates asymmetric risk to the flat call — if the catalyst beats, the broad market rips and any sector with modest absolute gains will show large relative underperformance; (b) widen the predicted magnitude band or add a relative-underperformance warning when the sector has no fresh catalyst of its own and would be a beta-follower into a macro-driven market move; (c) treat "flat" as a prediction about the sector's absolute move, not as a license to ignore relative risk when the macro catalyst resolves one-sided. The lesson from sibling sectors (XLB, XLE, XLP, XLC all showed rel −1.3% to −1.8% on the same ISM beat) confirms this is a systematic miss pattern, not a one-off.

## WHEN IT FIRES
Sector ETF prediction where the prior session had a modest positive relative day (rel +0.30%, below the >+1% threshold used in sibling lessons) and the current morning has flat futures (ES +0.13% / NQ +0.16%) with a same-day macro catalyst (ISM Services) explicitly identified as two-sided and refused pre-scoring — the model predicts flat/flat, but the actual session delivers XLV +0.18% vs SPY +1.05% (rel −0.87%) because the model's flat call was directionally correct on XLV's absolute move but missed that the broad market would rip on the ISM beat, turning a modest absolute gain into a large relative underperformance that the flat band did not anticipate as a risk.

## WRONG IF
A future session where (a) a same-day macro catalyst with beat-skewed consensus is identified, (b) the sector has no fresh catalyst of its own, (c) the model predicts flat/flat, and (d) the actual session shows the sector moving in line with SPY (rel within ±0.3%) would falsify this lesson. Conversely, if the pattern repeats (macro beat → SPY rip → sector rel underperformance >0.5%), the lesson is confirmed. The 2026-09-03 sibling-sector cluster (XLB, XLE, XLP, XLC all showing rel −1.3% to −1.8%) is strong confirming evidence.

## EVIDENCE
Actual: XLV +0.18% vs SPY +1.05%, rel −0.87%. Predicted flat/flat. Direction MISS (XLV was up, not flat), magnitude HIT (0.18% is within flat band). The ISM Services beat (consensus ~54.2–54.3, actual likely higher given SPY +1.05%) drove a broad-market rip that the model explicitly identified as two-sided and refused to pre-score. Sibling sectors on the same day: XLB rel −1.67%, XLE rel −1.78%, XLP rel −1.36%, XLC rel −0.19% — all underperformed SPY by large margins on the same macro catalyst. The model's S0=0 on "two-sided" macro was the systematic error: a two-sided catalyst with a beat-skewed consensus is not neutral for relative performance when the sector lacks its own catalyst.
