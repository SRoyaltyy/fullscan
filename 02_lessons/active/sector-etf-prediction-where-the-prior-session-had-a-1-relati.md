## RULE
When a sector has run 1w rel >+4% and 1m rel >+10%, and the current morning shows (a) the sector's own commodity offered (WTI −1.19%), (b) 1d rel already stalled at +0.07% (near zero after a big run), and (c) premarket breadth soft (XOM/CVX/COP red), the model should recognize this as an exhaustion/reversal setup and score S1=−2 (oil offered + extended-run mean reversion) rather than capping at −1 via the 08-12 continuation rule. The 08-12 rule (same geo narrative after 1w rel >+4% = continuation, not new shock) was designed to prevent double-counting a fresh surge, but it should not cap the downside when the commodity is actually falling and the tape is stalling. DO-INSTEAD should not override a genuine oil-offered + extended-run reversal signal into flat; it should only cap magnitude when the direction itself is ambiguous. When S1=−2 and S4=0 with a broad market ripping on macro (ISM beat), the correct call is down/mild-to-notable, not flat/flat.

## WHEN IT FIRES
Sector ETF prediction where the prior session had a >+1% relative gain (XLE 1w rel +4.40% / 1m rel +12.04%), the current morning has flat futures (ES +0.13% / NQ +0.16%) and the sector's own commodity (oil) is offered (WTI −1.19%), and the model scores S1=−1 on the oil factor but S4=0 on tape confirmation, then applies DO-INSTEAD to cap at flat/flat — but the actual session delivers XLE −0.74% vs SPY +1.05% (rel −1.78%) because the model treated the oil-offered sign as a mild −1 dampener rather than recognizing that an extended relative run (1w +4.4%, 1m +12%) combined with a stalled 1d tape (+0.07% rel) and a red premarket barrel is the classic exhaustion setup where the sector reverses hard against a broad-market rip.

## WRONG IF
A future session where XLE has 1w rel >+4%, oil is offered (WTI −1% to −2%), 1d rel is flat-to-slightly-positive, and XLE closes flat or positive (no reversal) would falsify this lesson. Also, a session where oil is offered but XLE has NOT had an extended run (1w rel <+2%) and the sector holds flat would confirm that the extended-run condition is necessary for the S1=−2 escalation.

## EVIDENCE
Actual XLE −0.737% vs SPY +1.047% (rel −1.784%). The model predicted flat/flat (total −2.7). The oil-offered sign (WTI −1.19%) was real and the sector reversed hard. The 08-12 continuation cap (S1 −1 not −2) was applied because "same geo narrative after 1w rel already >+4% — continuation, not a new shock" — but this rule was designed for the upside (preventing a fresh +2 surge score on leftover geo), and it was misapplied to cap the downside. The 08-27/08-28 leftover oil-down stack was dismissed as "does not fire" because oil was not collapsing, but the combination of extended relative performance + stalled tape + offered commodity is precisely the setup where a modest oil decline (−1.19%) triggers outsized sector underperformance (−1.78% rel). The model's own S4=0 (1d rel +0.07% flat) was read as confirmation of flat, when it should have been read as the first sign of momentum exhaustion after a +4.4% 1w run.
