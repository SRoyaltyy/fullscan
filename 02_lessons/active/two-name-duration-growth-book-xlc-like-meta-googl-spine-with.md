## RULE
Keep S0=0 when ES/NQ are mixed and the prior impulse is a non-holding (NVDA/XLK). Score yesterday’s two-name washout as history, not live breadth — S2=0 unless premarket dual-leaders are still failing. S4 may describe the prior close; it does not forecast the next session after a large lag with mixed-to-soft, not still-crashing, leaders. If S0+S1=0, do not let S2+S3+S4 carry a down call — default flat/mild. Do not treat no-divergence as confirmation when the leading sum is just S2/S3 copying S4. Split a scheduled Fed speech: inflation hawkishness ≠ XLC-down; do not pre-assign it to this book. Inverse of 08-11: prior-window outflows ≠ same-day drag.

## WHEN IT FIRES
Two-name duration/growth book (XLC-like: META+GOOGL spine) with S0=0 and S1=0 — mixed/non-transmitting futures, leftover impulse in a non-holding, no fresh ad/legal print — must not emit down just because yesterday’s large-cap failure sits in S2 and a large negative 1d relative print sits in S4. A ban on mapping foreign mega-cap beta into S0=+ is not a license to extrapolate the sector lag. Stale unit outflows are not same-day resistance. A two-sided policy speech is not an XLC-down spine; hawkish inflation can hit XLK/NVDA while ad/AI platforms mean-revert.

## WRONG IF
Next session with S0=S1=0, only stale S2/S4 lag, flat/mild call, and XLC still drops >1% on continuation of the same two-name fade with leaders still red into the open and no new shock. Or: this lesson is used to emit up after every washout and 08-27-type NVDA/XLK-only rallies miss again.
