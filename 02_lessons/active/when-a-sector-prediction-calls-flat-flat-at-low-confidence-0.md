## RULE
When S0 is negative due to a broad macro drag (oil shock + hawkish Fed + pre-CPI) that applies to the entire duration/growth book, and the S1 positive is a single-name catalyst (Meta legal relief) without breadth confirmation (GOOGL flat, NFLX down), the model must not treat them as exactly offsetting. The macro negative has broader reach (both META and GOOGL are duration assets; NFLX/DIS also rate-sensitive) while the sector positive is narrower (META only, ~17% weight). Score S1 lower (e.g., +0.5) when the positive is single-name without tape confirmation, or score S0 more negative when the macro drag is broad-based and the sector is a duration book. Additionally, when the model's own confidence is 0.5 and the tape is flat premarket but the macro setup (pre-CPI, oil elevated, hawkish Fed) has a known asymmetric-downside bias per the 09-04 lesson, the model should tilt toward down/mild rather than flat/flat — the asymmetric-downside lesson fires into the print window, and pre-CPI positioning days with oil elevated have historically shown negative drift for duration books.

## WHEN IT FIRES
When a sector prediction calls flat/flat at low confidence (0.5) into a pre-CPI day with an active oil-supply shock (day-2 continuation) and a hawkish Fed backdrop, the model treats S0=−1 and S1=+1 as exactly offsetting (net 0) despite the S1 positive being a single-name legal-overhang relief (Meta youth-safety deal) that is not confirmed by the tape (GOOGL flat, NFLX −5.4%) and the S0 negative being a broad macro drag affecting the entire duration/growth book. The model's "tug-of-war" framing fails to weight that the macro negative (oil + hawkish Fed + pre-CPI) applies to the full two-name book while the sector positive is a single-name catalyst with no breadth confirmation.

## WRONG IF
If a future pre-CPI day with oil elevated and hawkish Fed backdrop shows XLC flat or positive despite a single-name positive catalyst and no breadth confirmation, this lesson would be falsified. Conversely, if the model scores down/mild on such setups and XLC is flat or positive, the lesson needs revision.

## EVIDENCE
Actual XLC −0.62% vs SPY −0.46% → rel −0.15% (negative). Predicted flat/flat → direction MISS, magnitude MISS. The model's S0=−1/S1=+1 offset produced a flat call, but the actual showed XLC underperforming SPY by 15bp — consistent with the broad macro drag (oil + hawkish + pre-CPI) outweighing the single-name Meta catalyst. The 09-04 asymmetric-downside lesson (hot CPI more probable AND more damaging with hawkish Fed) should have tilted the pre-CPI positioning day negative, not flat. The model's own tape showed NFLX −5.4% (rate fears) — evidence that the duration/growth book was under pressure despite Meta's +4.6%, meaning the S1 positive was not broad enough to offset the macro drag.
