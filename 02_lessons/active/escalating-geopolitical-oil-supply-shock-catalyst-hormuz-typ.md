## RULE
When oil is up on an escalating supply shock but XLE is overbought/extended (RSI >70, 1w rel >+5%, already leading YTD) and the broad equity tape is risk-off or rotating into commodities rather than equities, the model should treat the overbought/crowding condition as a negative S2/S3 counterweight strong enough to offset the positive oil spine — producing at most flat, and likely down/relative-underperformance for XLE. If XLE direction is still called up, the magnitude should be capped at mild/notable with materially reduced confidence; if the broad tape is clearly risk-off into commodities, the direction should be down/flat.

## WHEN IT FIRES
Escalating geopolitical oil/supply-shock catalyst (Hormuz-type, Brent up >+1% and near multi-year highs) is present, but the energy equity ETF is extremely extended/overbought (RSI >70, 1w relative return >+5%) and the broad equity tape is turning risk-off into commodities while equities fade. In this setup, oil can rally while XLE closes flat-to-down as profit-taking and crowding unwind in the equity complex. An oil-up signal is not sufficient for an energy-equity-up call.

## WRONG IF
The lesson is falsified if, on a future session with oil up >+1%, XLE RSI >70 and 1w rel >+5%, and SPY/ES flat-to-down, XLE still closes >+0.5% with positive relative performance in at least 2 of 3 comparable episodes. That would show the decoupling is not reliable enough to override the oil spine.
