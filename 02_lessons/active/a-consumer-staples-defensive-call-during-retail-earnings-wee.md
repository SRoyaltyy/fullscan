## RULE
No correction required. Maintain the two-sided treatment of weak consumer data during retail-earnings week; count risk-off and flight-to-safety as one regime signal; require live top-holding/bellwether confirmation before converting a defensive bid into notable magnitude.

## WHEN IT FIRES
A consumer staples/defensive call during retail-earnings week with weak same-morning consumer data and a risk-off tape. The correct pattern is to treat the shared risk-off shock as a single flight-to-safety signal, not double-count weak consumer data as a pure tailwind, and to use live top-holding premarket strength or a sector bellwether beat as confirmation before allowing a notable magnitude.

## WRONG IF
A future identical setup — risk-off tape, Walmart premarket strength ahead of earnings, HD/consumer bellwether beat, S3/S4 confirming, weak macro data — that still produces an XLP absolute decline or SPY-relative underperformance would falsify the maintained weighting. Also, if weak consumer data is shown to be a dominant negative despite top-holding confirmation, the two-sided treatment would need revision.

## EVIDENCE
XLP +1.06% vs SPY -0.68% (rel +1.74%); direction and magnitude both HIT; risk-off flight-to-safety confirmed by the tech-led selloff and defensive rotation; Walmart strength ahead of Aug 20 earnings and HD’s beat were knowable at open and supported the call.
