## RULE
When a sector has a >+1% relative reversal day driven by a monetary-metals sleeve (gold/silver) rather than an industrial fundamental (copper squeeze, China rebound), treat the follow-through day as high-risk for reversal rather than confirmation. Specifically: (1) Do not score S1=+1 for a second consecutive day of gold/silver strength unless there is a NEW catalyst (fresh USD break, new real-yield move) — a second day of the same driver at similar magnitude is continuation risk, not confirmation; (2) When the prior day's reversal was >+1% relative and the sector's 1w relative is still negative (XLB 1w rel −1.08%), the bounce may be a dead-cat/positioning squeeze rather than a regime turn — require either 3d relative to be positive or a new fundamental catalyst before scoring up; (3) For a sector with a confirmed China/property demand contraction (NBS 49.8, construction 46.9, property FAI −19.2%), a gold-sleeve bounce should be capped at S1=0 or S1=+0.5 at most on the second day, because the monetary bid is a partial offset, not a sector driver; (4) When premarket gold is up ~1.5% for a second consecutive morning, check whether the prior day's XLB move already fully transmitted that gold gain — if XLB rose +1.69% on day 1 and gold is only repeating day-1's move, the marginal transmission to XLB on day 2 is near zero or negative (profit-taking).

## WHEN IT FIRES
A sector prediction scores a >+1% relative reversal day as "already in the tape" and therefore caps the follow-through at mild/confirmation-only, while the actual session reverses hard against the prior day's leadership (XLB +1.69% on 09-02 → XLB −0.62% / rel −1.67% on 09-03). The failure mode is treating a single-day bounce as a durable regime shift when the underlying drivers (gold sleeve, USD weakness) are themselves mean-reverting and the industrial demand backdrop (China contraction) remains the dominant weight.

## WRONG IF
A case where XLB has a >+1% relative reversal day driven by gold, followed by a second day where gold is again up ~1.5% premarket AND there is a NEW catalyst (e.g., fresh USD break below a key level, new real-yield decline, or a China stimulus announcement) — in that case, S1=+1 and S4=+1 would be appropriate and the rule would incorrectly cap the call. Also falsified if XLB's 1w relative is already positive (not −1.08%) entering the follow-through day, indicating the bounce has legs.

## EVIDENCE
Actual 09-03: XLB −0.62% vs SPY +1.05%, relative −1.67% — a complete reversal of the prior day's +1.25% relative gain. The prediction's S1=+1 (gold sleeve) and S4=+1 (confirmation) both failed. The 1w relative was −1.08% entering 09-03, meaning the 09-02 bounce only partially recovered a multi-day underperformance — the sector was still in a downtrend against SPY, and the bounce was not sufficient to flip the intermediate trend. The prediction's own research noted "gold back above $4,400" and "yesterday XLB +1.69% was the gold-transmission day" — but then scored the second day as if the transmission would repeat at full strength. The outcome shows the gold sleeve faded into the cash session while SPY rallied (risk-on tape), and XLB underperformed by −1.67% relative — consistent with profit-taking in the prior day's leaders during a broad risk-on day.
