## RULE
When a >+1% relative reversal occurs but (a) the sector's 1w relative performance is still negative by >1%, (b) the catalyst is a sleeve (gold/silver) rather than the sector's core driver (copper/industrial metals), and (c) the fundamental spine (China, inventory, supply) is unchanged or still negative — treat the >+1% reversal as a potential exhaustion bounce within a downtrend, not as confirmation of a new up-leg. Score S1=0 (not +1) when the sleeve catalyst is continuation and the core industrial factors are still negative; score S4=0 (not +1) when the 1d reversal is the only tape evidence and 3d/1w relative are still negative. The follow-through rule should be symmetric: it caps magnitude AND it should reduce directional conviction when the reversal is sleeve-driven within a negative 1w context. Default to flat/neutral rather than up when the only positive is a continuation sleeve and the core sector drivers are unchanged-negative.

## WHEN IT FIRES
Sector ETF prediction where a >+1% relative reversal follow-through day is followed by a morning where (a) futures are flat-to-barely-green, (b) the prior day's catalyst (gold/silver) is still bid but is continuation not a fresh squeeze, and (c) the sector has a 1w relative performance still negative — the model scores S1=+1 on the monetary-metals sleeve and S4=+1 on tape confirmation, but the actual session reverses hard (XLB −0.62% vs SPY +1.05%, rel −1.67%) because the follow-through rule was applied asymmetrically: it was used to cap magnitude at mild but not to question whether the prior day's >+1% relative move was itself the exhaustion event rather than the start of a new up-leg.

## WRONG IF
A day where XLB has >+1% 1d rel reversal, 1w rel still negative >1%, gold/silver bid, China/copper flat-to-negative — and XLB still goes UP the next day by >0.5% relative. If that happens, the rule would have wrongly defaulted to flat. However, the 09-03 outcome (rel −1.67%) is a strong falsifier of the CURRENT behavior (scoring up), so the corrected behavior is better calibrated.

## EVIDENCE
Predicted up/mild (total 3.15, S1=+1 on gold/silver, S4=+1 on 1d rel +1.25%) vs actual XLB −0.62%, SPY +1.05%, rel −1.67%. The gold/silver sleeve was indeed bid (+1.48%/+1.71% per Channel 1) but XLB fell anyway — the monetary-metals sleeve does not carry XLB when the industrial core (copper +0.11%, China contraction, LME stocks rebuilt) is flat-to-negative. The 1w relative was −1.08% entering the day, meaning the >+1% 1d reversal on 09-02 was a bounce within a downtrend, not an inflection. SPY +1.05% on 09-03 shows broad risk-on that XLB did not participate in — the sector's relative weakness was not a risk-off tape issue but a sector-specific mean-reversion.
