## RULE
For a defensive sector, first check catalyst freshness. If the negative catalyst is ≥1 session old and the same-morning reversal checklist is positive, treat the negative as already reflected in the tape and do not keep it as a fresh S1 negative. On a recovery day after a yield-driven sell-off, staples and health care can lead even with positive futures, so positive futures do not automatically mean defensives underperform. Use prior-session evidence — e.g., XLP holding flat while WMT fell ~9% — as confirmation that the broad defensive bid is strong. Score S1 at 0 or positive, let S2/S4 confirm, reconcile the deterministic output with the narrative, and lean flat-to-up/mild instead of down/flat.

## WHEN IT FIRES
A defensive/bond-proxy sector is predicted down/flat because (1) a top-holding bellwether dropped sharply in the prior session, (2) a prior-day hawkish Fed-minutes headline is still being treated as a live negative, and (3) positive futures are assumed to mean defensives will lag. Meanwhile the same-morning reversal checklist is positive — futures ≥ +0.3%, real yields easing, oil not spiking — and the prior session’s tape already absorbed the top-holding drop without the sector falling. The stale negative is over-weighted, causing a missed defensive flight-to-safety up day.

## WRONG IF
A future session with a positive same-morning reversal checklist but a fresh same-morning negative catalyst — e.g., a new top-holding earnings miss reported that morning, or a same-day hard-data miss — that still produces a down/flat sector close would falsify this lesson. A prior-session echo of an old negative does not falsify it; the lesson only discards stale catalysts.

## EVIDENCE
2026-08-21 XLP +0.785% vs SPY +0.409% (rel +0.376); predicted down/flat. WMT fell ~9% on Aug 20 but was roughly flat on Aug 21; JPMorgan called the drop a buying opportunity, Walmart announced price cuts on 11,000 items using a $2.9B tariff refund, defensive staples and health care outperformed, and staples funds drew inflows. Before Aug 21, XLP was +0.07% on the day WMT fell ~9% — showing the broad sector bid was already absorbing the top-holding drag.
