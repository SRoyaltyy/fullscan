## RULE
Cap any sector-specific-drag override of a regime signal at ONE session. To keep the drag at full weight on a subsequent day, require a FRESH negative print (new guidance cut, new data, new downgrade) that morning; absent one, the drag reverts to a carried structural weight (≈ half). When the regime panel is maximal-FTS, a defensive sector's trailing relative lag is a SETUP for mean-reversion/FTS catch-up, not a bearish confirmation — score S4 at 0 or slightly positive, not negative. When de-duplicating a two-sided shock (oil = input-cost negative AND haven bid), count it ONCE at the correct NET weight; never keep only the negative leg and drop the positive leg. Apply the 08-18 template explicitly: rising long-end yields + risk-off → relative outperformance / flat-to-marginally-positive absolute.

## WHEN IT FIRES
A sector-specific negative cluster (single-sleeve drag, e.g. packaged-food margin/dividend stress) has printed negative for 2–3 consecutive sessions, and the model converts that streak into a standing "dominance rule" that zeroes or negates a maximal, live, market-wide flight-to-safety regime signal (VIX backwardation + oil >$100 + NQ lagging + Asia red + strongly negative 10Y–SPX correlation) at the open of the next session — even though no fresh sector-specific negative print exists that morning.

## WRONG IF
If, on a future session with a maximal-FTS panel (VIX backwardation + oil >$100 + NQ lagging + Asia red + 10Y–SPX corr ≤ −0.9) and a carried (non-fresh) sector-specific negative cluster, the defensive sector ETF closes DOWN in absolute terms and underperforms SPY, then the one-session cap and the "trailing lag = setup not confirmation" rule are wrong and the 09-09 dominance rule should be restored to full weight.

## EVIDENCE
2026-09-10 XLP closed +0.048% vs SPY −0.599% (rel +0.648%) — a relative HIT and absolute-sign MISS. The morning memo itself wrote the correct 08-18 template ("rising 10Y + risk-off → relative outperformance / flat-to-negative absolute; do not upgrade to absolute up") and then scored −2.925 against it. It also wrote "Risk-off + NQ lag + oil >$100 = theoretical relative FTS bid" and then zeroed it. The food-crash cluster produced NO fresh negative on 09-10 — the streak went quiet, which is exactly why the FTS bid lifted the ETF. XLP gapped UP at the open (83.37 vs prior close ~83.05) into a risk-off tape — the FTS bid was visible at the open and the memo used the prior day's close-to-close rel instead.
