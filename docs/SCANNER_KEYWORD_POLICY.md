# Concrete company connections

The scanner's public keyword view and retrieval index use `concrete-connections-2` in `src/stock_keywords.py`. This contract applies to every issuer. It is a conservative admission filter over extracted records, not a claim of complete or 90% accurate extraction.

Version 2 requires the relationship capacity in the named argument's own sentence and prose block. A customer cue in the next paragraph cannot turn a product table into customers. Participant lists, appositions and direct purchase/sale/partner constructions are checked separately. Laws cannot be subsidiaries or business counterparties; risk events cannot be upstream inputs; company boards are not government institutions. Common input classes, workforce classes, financial quantities, filing fragments and incomplete legal names are excluded. This adds no newly fetched web sources and does not replace the immutable source records.

The stock view consolidates duplicate records only under the same existing identity, direction, legal scope and temporal status. It retains all evidence IDs, source names and dates, and keeps different roles separate. Every admitted record is searchable; pagination imposes no output cap. A resolved identity is required to consolidate aliases; unresolved concepts are never merged by similar wording.

A candidate needs an identifiable name, statutory citation, source-declared identity, or qualified operating input; a supported relationship capacity; and dated, exact source evidence. Named entities and specific inputs can be plural. Pinned WordNet physical-material, artifact, food, plant and animal categories also admit single-word operating inputs without a ticker or commodity allowlist; article identity and sense checks remain separate. Capitalization or an assigned entity type alone does not rescue generic participant classes, abstract objects, administrative terms, page/table fragments, incomplete arguments or quantities without a named instrument. Commercial offerings require commercial source context. A country is not admitted as a subsidiary or counterparty.

Explicitly negated relationships and explicit legal scope exclusions are null connections. They cannot appear in keyword results, including unresolved and rejected result lists. Dated termination events also close older positive versions of the same connection. A supported historical connection can still be tested at a date before its termination; same-date contradictions abstain. An exclusion does not erase independent relationships to other entities in the sentence.

The public view groups accepted records by upstream suppliers, downstream customers, business partners, operating dependencies, upstream inputs, products/services, competitors, subsidiaries, acquisitions/divestitures, officers, government institutions, laws/policies, named legal proceedings, permits/licences and jurisdictions. Labels preserve direction: buys from, sells to, relies on, partners with, offers, and so forth. A named legal exposure is labelled 'is subject to' only when the disclosure record explicitly asserts a direct obligation; other qualifying mentions retain an exposure label and review requirement.

Rejected candidates remain in immutable source archives for evaluation. The scanner does not render the old generic category chips or raw grammar relationship list. Whole source paragraphs remain available for evidence review; their wording is not a keyword connection list.

This filter does not resolve every named entity, repair every old grammatical attachment, certify a law's applicability, or infer trading impact. It does not add external licence/court/regulator coverage. Existing review requirements and typed article identity gates remain in force. Benchmark extraction recall and precision must be measured separately from filtering tests.
