# Deterministic relevance coverage matrix

Version CM-2. This is a failure model, not a claim that every sentence or world fact is covered. Runtime decisions use no LLM. Tests establish their stated behavior. Global geography, historical ownership and general grammar remain partial.

| Category | Evidence required | Decision rule | Positive example | Confusing negative | Missing evidence | Coverage |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|INPUT_EMPTY|Article text or a meaningful headline|Return insufficient_text; count a missed positive as a failure|$AAPL raises guidance.|Markets moved.|Abstain; retain input-quality reason|implemented|
|INPUT_DUPLICATED|Distinct title/body passages|Deduplicate passages before extracting evidence|Headline followed by independent reporting|Headline pasted twice as its body|Use headline evidence only|implemented|
|INPUT_TRUNCATED|Complete scanner body and complete source views|Do not apply character caps; expose upstream missing data|Relevant event after character 20,000|An excerpt falsely labeled complete|Mark incomplete; fail strict archive mode|implemented|
|SPELLING|Pinned spelling and lemma resources|Normalize spelling consistently on both sides|defence / defense|hour / our|Keep unrecognized words unchanged|implemented|
|INFLECTION|spaCy English lemma lookup plus dictionary fallback|Map inflections to lemmas, preserving the surface in source evidence|acquired / acquire|Lockheed / lockh|Keep unknown forms distinct|implemented|
|DERIVATION|Explicit contextual lexical relation|Do not collapse different derivatives into identity|administer associated with administration in a supporting context|administrator treated as the same object as administration|No unsupported credit|conservative|
|WORD_SENSE|WordNet sense gloss plus article context|Select one strictly best-supported sense; synonyms alone cannot create multiple independent anchors|river water and bank|bank branch and riverbank|Keep literal term; abstain if ambiguous|implemented|
|ORTHOGRAPHIC_NEIGHBOR|Dictionary identity or explicit semantic relation|No spelling-distance expansion|frog / amphibian in a matching sense|frogman automatically treated as frog|No relationship inferred|implemented|
|PHRASE_BOUNDARY|Contiguous tokens in the same sentence|Keep phrases; never join across punctuation or stopword boundaries|space transportation|rocket. Gasoline|Fall back to individual terms|implemented|
|LOCAL_CONTEXT|Two locally supported business anchors|Match evidence per passage, not a mixed article-wide bag|rocket launch expands|office space; school launches a program|Sector context or no supported stock link|implemented|
|VERB_NOUN|Local passage context|A verb occurrence cannot delete a noun elsewhere|plan to launch a website; rocket launch capacity expands|launch alone implies aerospace|Require corroborating business terms|implemented|
|PUBLISHER_ATTRIBUTION|Publisher catalog and raw case-preserving identities|Publisher tokens cannot create name hits|$POST raises earnings guidance|Washington Post reports local crime|No entity link|implemented|
|ENTITY_COLLISION|Company identity plus financial/business context|Do not equate a foreign exchange symbol or agency with a US company|NYSE:ICE earnings|ICE arrests; Kosy (KOS) in Vietnam|Inspection or no name hit|implemented_with_known_exchange_guards|
|SHORT_NAME|Unique raw company prefix plus a local event; dictionary words need corporate or financial grammar|Preserve punctuation and ADR identity; reject place prefixes, foreign qualifiers, compound suffixes, and blank-check shorthand|Taiwan Semiconductor results; PG&E shutoff; Arm stock|New Jersey man; National Bank Of Hungary; Character.AI|Inspection or no entity link|implemented|
|OWNED_PRODUCT|Ownership passage in the stock source plus article event|Use the complete product identifier and unique evidenced owner|VOXZOGO event to BMRN|VOXZOGO silently becomes an ASND product|Inspection if ownership ambiguous|implemented|
|PRODUCT_COMPONENT|Complete brand phrase|Do not turn each acronym inside a product name into an owned brand|TransCon CNP|CNP alone treated as the owned branded product|Candidate only|implemented|
|SUBSIDIARY|Exhibit 21 or explicit subsidiary ownership statement|A subsidiary event can reach its evidenced parent|LinkedIn layoffs to MSFT|A partner's product treated as an owned subsidiary|Inspection if owner not established|implemented|
|PERSON_ALIAS|Named person in filings plus separate business evidence|People trigger inspection; require corroborating business anchors|Musk and spacecraft launch context|Musk mentioned without a company event|Inspection only|implemented|
|ACRONYM_ALIAS|Local first-use definition|Store a typed acronym; it does not bypass relevance evidence|Defined platform acronym in a business event|FDA inferred to be a listed company's ticker|Inspection only|implemented|
|RELATION_ROLE|Source section and relationship cue|Keep customers, suppliers, partners and competitors out of owned core identity|Our supplier is Microsoft|Microsoft mention turns supplier's whole business into software|Preserve typed relation, no direct pass|implemented|
|COMPETITOR_EXPOSURE|Explicit competition evidence, shared business exposure and a specific competitive event|Competition may be material; do not confuse it with product ownership|A rival achondroplasia drug approval|Any competitor name anywhere passes|Inspection when material event not established|implemented|
|DEPENDENCY_MATERIALITY|Explicit sole/critical dependency plus actual disruptive event|Material supply events can pass as tier 2; ordinary partner mentions remain tier 4|Sole supplier halts production|Partner merely mentioned in a gambling article|Second-order record only|implemented|
|GEOGRAPHIC_SCOPE|Named place and dated operating exposure|Place is a scope/inspection signal, never sufficient evidence|Rule affecting an evidenced operating jurisdiction|Charlotte police implies Duke Energy|Inspection; do not infer operational exposure from an absent source|partial_geographic_inventory|
|EVENT_SCOPE|Action and matching business exposure in the same passage|Do not combine unrelated clauses to meet the pass threshold|Launch infrastructure policy and launch-services business|A transport office title implies an airline event|No supported link|implemented_local_scope|
|NEGATION|Company is the actor or patient of a reported event|A denial or rejection remains related; polarity is separate|Microsoft denies acquisition report|Drop a company merely because event is negated|Retain relatedness evidence|implemented|
|COMPARISON|Local comparison/attribution cue|A comparison name is not automatically the subject of the event|Company's own earnings report|Unlike Microsoft, university raises tuition|Mention only|implemented_local_guards|
|TEMPORAL_SOURCE|Filing dates and explicit as-of cutoff|Exclude future filings and reject future-dated source evidence|October event supported by September filing|October filing explains a July article in a backtest|Fail/abstain when dated proof is unavailable|implemented_filing_dates|
|OWNERSHIP_CHANGE|Dated acquisition/divestiture state|Do not infer current product ownership from a historical statement|Current owned product|Previously divested business remains an owned alias|Require current ownership proof|partial_historical_state|
|INDUSTRY_VS_STOCK|Separate company and industry vocabularies|Industry terms support sector context; specific stock links need separate evidence|Specific therapy event|drug/readout passes eight unrelated biotechs|Tier-3 industry context|implemented|
|RARE_IDENTIFIER|A unique product/business term with provenance|Do not penalize an identifier for appearing in few industry members|VOXZOGO in a large biotech industry|Discard it because industry coverage is low|Keep as candidate/owned evidence|implemented|
|EVIDENCE_DOUBLE_COUNT|Source-term identity across phrases/synonyms|One underlying word cannot become three independent confirmations|Two distinct business anchors|drug + drug synonym + same word in industry gate|Insufficient corroboration|implemented|
|BATCH_INSTABILITY|Pair-specific evidence independent of comparison pool|The same article/company pair must retain its decision under batch duplication/order changes|One or nine copies of the space memo|Crowded news day suppresses every pass|No batch-relative veto|implemented|
|OUTPUT_TRUNCATION|Complete returned relevance set|Do not cut passes to a display top-12/top-24 limit|All evidenced affected stocks returned|Valid link removed by UI row cap|Separate presentation limits from decision output|implemented|
|EXTRA_FALSE_LINK|Complete expected ticker set|Every unexpected pass is a false positive|Exactly the labeled set|Expected RKLB plus unrelated AAL accepted|Fail the case|implemented|
|FALSE_UNKNOWN|A labeled positive case|Unknown on a positive is a missed link, never a successful safe answer|Thin but explicit earnings headline|Abstain on every meaningful article|Count false negative and abstention|implemented|
|PARTIAL_LABEL|Explicit truth_complete and label provenance|Partial regression assertions cannot certify accuracy|Complete independently reviewed expected set|Negative-only bug assertion reported as overall accuracy|Exclude and disclose denominator|implemented|
|SOURCE_COMPLETENESS|Archive manifest, expected filing count, text checksums|Fail strict mode on incomplete/missing/corrupt text; label legacy caches incomplete|Complete PR642 text views|Capped SQLite cache presented as the full archive|Stop strict ingestion and report missing sources|implemented|
|VALIDATION_DISTRIBUTION|Positive/negative and per-category metrics|Report precision, recall, exact-set accuracy and coverage; no all-negative certification|At least 20 positive cases and diverse examples|100 irrelevant articles return unknown|No relevance bar claim|implemented|
|UNCLASSIFIED_FAILURE|An observed mismatch outside this matrix|Record a new category and lock a case before changing the implementation|New observed error with a reproducible case|Silently patch an individual word|Block a claimed comprehensive release|process_gate|
|BODY_PROVENANCE|Publisher-body provenance rather than text length|Extended text is an unverified sampling stratum; export primary headlines separately|Headline-only mode excludes related-story bundle|Forty words of concatenated search headlines called a genuine body|Disclose unverified provenance|partial_upstream_provenance|
|BUSINESS_PARAGRAPH|Contiguous short business paragraph in one source and section|Allow two rare anchors across a paragraph of at most 100 tokens; retain all original sentence facts|Launch services and spacecraft explained in adjacent sentences|Business and risk sections joined as evidence|Require a single valid witness|implemented|
|UNKNOWN_EVENT_TARGET|Identified company/product or broad business constraint|An isolated unnamed incident cannot pass every business selling that class of object|Sourced company event or launch infrastructure policy|Anonymous retail-store arson passes apparel retailers|Tier-3 context or no supported link|implemented_local_scope|
|RAW_PRODUCT_IDENTITY|Raw brand surface and a valid ownership predicate|Normalize plural brands only through the known product inventory; distinguish case and company-name constituents|Macs versus MAC cosmetics|AML treatment target or IPO service becomes owned product|No product pass without ownership proof|implemented|
|OFFLINE_RUNTIME|Pinned local resources and isolated interpreter|Evaluator disables site packages, DNS, and socket connections before importing application code|python -I -S scripts/offline_evidence_checks.py|Runtime calls an external inference or telemetry endpoint|Fail rather than fetch at scan time|implemented|
