## 5. LESSONS / CALIBRATION

1. **The extra-confirm gate is asymmetric in a risk-on regime.** The gate was designed to prevent over-weighting a *down* smash after a paid down-twin. But in a strong risk-on tape, the gate should not prevent *any* negative S0 weight. The morning's own text said "0 to slightly negative" and then scored 0. **Fix: when the tape is a ≥0.5% rip with NQ leading and the sector is PM red, S0 should be −0.5 to −1, not 0.**

2. **Ex-dividend days need a magnitude adjustment, not just a scoring note.** The morning correctly excluded ex-div from factor scoring but did not adjust the magnitude band. On a ~0.73% ex-div day, a "mild" absolute prediction should be understood as "mild total-return, possibly notable printed." **Fix: add a printed-vs-total-return flag to the magnitude band on ex-div days.**

3. **When rates ease and the sector still falls, the rotation factor is dominant.** The morning identified this correctly in S1 but under-weighted it in S0. The diagnostic — "yields moved in the sector's favor and it fell anyway" — is a strong signal that rotation is the only game in town.

4. **The direction was right; the magnitude was understated.** This is a *calibration* miss, not a *directional* miss. The engine's rolling dir=0.4 / mag=0.2 (n=10) reflects this pattern. The fix is in the magnitude band, not the sign.
