## 5. Lessons to carry forward

1. **Asymmetric factors must not be netted to zero.** When one factor is "ban on up" and another is "ban on down," the correct output is not automatically flat — check whether both actually point the same way for the *relative* series. Here, NQ-lead + oil-down + yields-down all pointed to **XLF relative-down**, and the card scored them 0.
2. **A flat absolute call in a strong index tape is a relative miss, and the rubric should say so.** The morning card's own HIT_GRID listed "Sector rotation out of financials" as PARTIAL — the grid saw it, the score did not.
3. **Persistent negative relative momentum (3d/1w/1m all red) is a live lid in risk-on tapes**, even if it is not a lid in flat tapes. The 08-28 lesson needs a regime qualifier.
4. **The 08-27 lesson (NQ/XLK lead = inverse of rotation-into-banks) should carry negative S0 weight, not zero weight**, when the lead is ≥ ~0.5% and the sector's own PM is ≤ 0.
5. **Keep the good habits:** refusing to restack the paid FOMC, refusing to score oil as a financials positive, refusing to let the ES=F sleeve flip the card, and refusing to import the general "follow ES/NQ ≥ +0.5%" rule onto XLF. All four were correct and all four prevented a *worse* miss.
