## 5. Lessons / rule updates

1. **The 09-24 "don't pay the minority sleeve" rule needs a precondition guard.** It should only bind when the **commodity sleeve is flat-to-red**. On 10-08 the sleeve was green (Cu +0.66%, GC +0.90%, SI +1.96%) and the rule suppressed a correct +1. **Amend: if the metals sleeve is green ≥ +0.5% across ≥ 2 legs, the sleeve is payable regardless of book composition.**

2. **A green anchor must not be overridden by a red narrative.** The engine anchor was **+0.192**; the overlay was **−4.037**. When the anchor and the overlay disagree in *sign*, the overlay should be capped, not free. This is the single largest source of the −5.542 miss.

3. **The 1m rel hole is a mean-reversion input, not a continuation input.** The morning's own HORIZON_2W note said so; the S3 score ignored it. **Promote the horizon insight into the S3 score.**

4. **HEAT maps are pre-open snapshots and must be re-validated against the cash open.** Aluminum was "down, 0% breadth" in HEAT and **+1.10%** in cash. A HEAT-vs-cash contradiction of this size should trigger a breadth re-score, not a confirmation.

5. **The 10-02 lesson was misapplied.** The morning correctly noted the 10-02 "don't sign down" protection was OFF (binary printed, tape red). But it then **signed down anyway** — and the *reason* the 10-02 rule existed (don't fight a green commodity tape) was exactly the condition present on 10-08. The rule's *trigger* was off; its *wisdom* was on.
