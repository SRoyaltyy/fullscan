## Lesson (for the Energy book)

After a multi-day oil-down stack that **already missed magnitude** (08-27), a still-red but **sub-1% WTI open is not a down spine**. Count oil **once**, and only if it is still transmitting into the cash session. Extreme cracks are not a footnote when RBOB is the live mover — they can flip XLE even with soft crude. Do not copy yesterday’s relative tape into S4. Warsh-type policy binaries are S0 two-sided: they can **bid XLE vs SPY** rather than press oil. Next time this setup prints, default **flat/mild** unless CL/BZ are still ≥~1% down *and* XOM/COP are confirming premarket.
