## 5. LESSONS FOR THE NEXT SESSION

1. **On a CPI risk-on day, do not score a defensive sector as a beneficiary.** Risk-on is a **rotation signal**, and healthcare is a **funding source**, not a destination. S0 for XLV on a green-tape CPI day should be **0 to −0.3**, not +0.5.
2. **"Stabilization" after a multi-day relative decline is not exhaustion unless flow confirms.** Two small positive relative prints (+0.14%, +0.05%) followed by a −1.03% relative print is a **lower-high**, not a base. Require a **positive 1d relative print on above-average volume** before calling exhaustion.
3. **Flat 1m relative ≠ demand.** Absence of crowding is not presence of buyers. S3 should score **negative** when a sector has given back outperformance but shows no fresh inflow.
4. **A defensive sector gapping up on a risk-on macro print is a sell-the-gap candidate.** The morning had this setup and mis-signed it.
5. **Correlated errors are worse than divergences.** When leading sum and tape agree, the framework produces false confidence. Add a **contrarian check**: "what would make this sector *underperform* a green tape?" — and if the answer is "rotation out," score it.
