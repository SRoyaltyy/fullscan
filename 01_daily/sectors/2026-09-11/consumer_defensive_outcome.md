## 5. LESSONS AND FORWARD ADJUSTMENTS

**Lesson 1 (scoring symmetry):** When a positive sector factor is scored (input-cost relief +0.5), a **comparable negative factor identified in the same analysis** (risk-on rotation) must be **scored symmetrically**, not merely described. The packet's prose correctly flagged the rotation as a relative headwind but left it unscored, producing an upward-biased total. **DO-INSTEAD:** if a factor is named as a "headwind" or "drag" in the prose, it must receive a **negative score** in the corresponding component, or the prose must be removed. No unscored named factors.

**Lesson 2 (relative vs absolute):** The packet's output format predicts **absolute direction and magnitude band** — and on those terms, this was a **HIT** (up/flat, +0.35%). But the **relative** outcome (−0.50%) was the more informative result, and the packet's slight up bias did not anticipate it. **DO-INSTEAD:** when the packet's own analysis identifies a **risk-on-leaning tape**, the relative lean should be explicitly **negative for defensives**, even when the absolute lean is positive. Consider emitting a **relative direction** field alongside the absolute one.

**Lesson 3 (CPI in-line resolution):** The packet's S0=0 for a two-sided CPI binary was **correct** — CPI came in line and the binary resolved neutrally. This **validates** the 08-12 rule (do not force S0 negative merely because CPI exists) and the 09-04 refinement (asymmetry is milder when the live curve is flat-to-easing). **Keep this discipline.**

**Lesson 4 (S4 discipline vindicated):** The packet's refusal to carry the 09-10 +0.65% rel into a second up day (S4=0) was **exactly right** — XLP reversed to −0.50% rel. The 08-14 outsized-reversal rule and the 09-10 lesson both worked. **Keep this discipline.**

**Lesson 5 (pipeline bug):** The JSON `total_score` (1.35) did not match the narrative Σ×mult (+0.45). This is an **aggregation inconsistency** that should be investigated — it implies the pipeline may be summing absolute values or double-counting. **Flag for pipeline review.**
