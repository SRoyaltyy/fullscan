## 6. Lessons

1. **The empty-spine gate was over-applied.** Its precondition (net ≈ 0 **and no divergence**) was not met — S4 was +0.5 and a divergence existed. When the gate's precondition fails, **defer to the engine**, don't suppress it. This is the **third consecutive session** (09-23, 09-24, 09-25) where the gate/emit-cap family produced a **direction miss** — twice by suppressing up, once by suppressing down. The family is **too aggressive**.
2. **Nested-heat rules need a netting step, not just a suppression step.** "Do not average nested heat into the parent" is right for *scoring*; it is wrong as a *complete* treatment. The correct move is: **score nested heat at 0, then ask whether the nested bid is large enough to move the parent's absolute.** Here it was (Plans lifted XLV to +0.49%); it was not large enough to move relative.
3. **Single-day relative cushions (S4) should not be read as persistent.** The +0.72% 1d rel from 09-24 fully decayed to −0.05%. The 08-28 anti-stacking rule is **confirmed again**.
4. **Plans/Facilities OVERRIDE heat is a real XLV absolute driver.** Two consecutive signals (MAP HEAT Plans up/med, Facilities up/high) plus a same-day Barclays upgrade produced a **+7% single-name move**. This cluster deserves a **small positive S1 weight for the absolute**, explicitly netted against non-Plans drag for the relative.
