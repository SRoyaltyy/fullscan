## 2. Audit of morning S0–S4 reads against reality

I use the **morning numbers as published**, not post-close rewrites.

### S0_SHARED_MACRO: morning +1.0 → **verdict: PARTIAL / over-scored on the relative leg**

**What actually happened:** the rates configuration did **not** produce a relative defensive bid. XLP underperformed SPY by 10 bp. The 09-23 setup (XLP +1.34% rel on a rates-led risk-off day) did **not** repeat.

**Was the morning read defensible?** Partially. The card was right that a non-print is not evidence of absence, and right not to zero S0. But it **conflated two distinct claims**:
1. "The rates configuration is live and directional" — **true**.
2. "It is directional *for XLP relative*" — **false today**.

The 09-23 lesson was about *not marking the spine MISS on a non-print*. The card over-extended it into *scoring the spine positive on a non-print*. The correct 09-23-corrected read was: **leave the spine unsigned (0), not positive (+1)**. The card's own language — "at minimum leave the FTS spine unsigned rather than MISS" — literally names the correct action, then does something stronger.

**This is the core error.** The card's S0 = +1 was a *relative* claim dressed as an *absolute* claim. The absolute leg resolved up (correct), but for beta reasons, not rates reasons. The relative leg resolved down (incorrect).

## 3. Interactions / double-count / knowable-at-open test

**Double-count audit (morning):** The card was disciplined — it counted the 10Y>5.2% / Williams / Warsh / gold cluster **once** in S0, kept oil's input-cost leg in S1, and did not restack the rotation-out into S1. **No double-count error.** This is a genuine strength of the card.

**But there was a subtler interaction error:** the card treated the rates object as *unambiguously positive for XLP* while simultaneously noting that **equity futures were green and Europe was green**. Those two facts are in tension. A rates-led risk-off produces a defensive relative bid *only if the equity tape is risk-off*. With ES +0.28%, NQ +0.57%, and Europe +0.64%, the tape was **risk-on**, which means the rates back-up was being absorbed as a *growth-tolerant* move, not a *haven-demand* move. The card noted this ("this is not a broad risk-off tape") but did not let it reduce S0 below +1. **The green tape should have been the tiebreaker against the relative leg.**

**Knowable-at-open test:** Was the relative miss knowable at the open?
- The green futures/Europe tape: **yes, knowable**.
- The non-haven PM (−0.12%, mid/bottom of a green book): **yes, knowable**.
- The negative rotation signal (09-17): **yes, knowable**.
- The COST sell-the-news: **no, not knowable** — but the card correctly flagged COST as two-sided event risk and refused to pre-score it. That discipline was right.

**Verdict: PARTIALLY knowable.** The three inputs that pointed against the relative leg were all visible at the open. The card saw all three, named all three, and then let S0 = +1 override them. The absolute direction (up/mild) was the correct headline; the relative lean was the knowable error.

## 6. Lesson for the next Consumer Defensive card

**When the rates object is the load-bearing S0 input, the relative leg requires a risk-off equity tape to fire.** A 10Y back-up with green futures and green Europe is a *growth-tolerant* rates move — it lifts beta, not defensives' relative performance. The 09-23 FTS bid fired because the tape was risk-off; today's did not because the tape was risk-on. **The correct S0 score today was +0.5 (absolute up via beta, relative unsigned), not +1.0.** The card's own divergence flag and its own "not a haven print" PM observation were the correct signals, and they should have capped S0 at +0.5 and zeroed the relative lean.

The 09-23 correction ("don't mark the spine MISS on a non-print") was applied correctly as *discipline* but over-applied as *direction*. The distinction: **a non-print means "unsigned," not "positive."**
