ERROR_CATEGORY: B — reasoning/weighting error, not tool/data failure. All necessary inputs (positive futures, easing real yields, stale macro headline, negative medium-term relative tape) were available at open.

TRIGGER_PATTERN:  
After a sharp prior-session risk-off selloff, the next morning has a positive same-morning reversal checklist — US index futures, especially NQ, ≥ +0.3%; real yields easing; no fresh same-day negative hard-data/earnings catalyst; and the dominant macro negative (hawkish Fed minutes, yields spike, stagflation fears) is 1–2 sessions old and already absorbed in the prior tape. The model nevertheless keeps S0 negative and predicts down/flat because the sector’s 1w/1m relative tape is negative and the macro narrative is still prominent. The sector then gaps up, fades, closes flat-to-up but underperforms SPY.

CURRENT_BEHAVIOR:  
Retains a stale macro headwind as a live S0 negative even when the same-morning tape is a relief bounce; describes the divergence as “mild” but does not let it change direction; converts “tech will underperform SPY” into an absolute down call. Result: predicted down/flat, actual up/flat — direction miss, magnitude hit.

CORRECTED_BEHAVIOR:  
Run the reversal checklist independently. If the macro negative is not fresh (1+ sessions old), there is no fresh same-day negative catalyst, and futures are clearly positive, do not let the stale macro headline keep S0 at -1. Downgrade S0 to neutral or allow the positive futures to set the direction. Keep the magnitude capped at flat/mild if the medium-term relative tape is negative and a top-weight catalyst (e.g., Nvidia earnings) is pending. The correct call in this setup is typically up/flat while still expecting underperformance versus SPY — not down/flat.
