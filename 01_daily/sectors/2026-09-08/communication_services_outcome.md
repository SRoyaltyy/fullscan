**Telecom components may have provided a modest cushion.** With TMUS noted as positive in the morning map heat, and telecom being a defensive/rate-sensitive sub-sector, any telecom strength would have partially offset weakness in the growth/ad names.

**SPY fell more than XLC — the relative winner was the sector with the duration/growth profile.** This is the key surprise: on a day when the morning analysis argued XLC would underperform due to duration sensitivity + rising real yields, the actual tape showed XLC outperforming. This suggests either (a) the real-yield move was not as punishing as feared, or (b) the risk-off was broad-based enough that mega-cap quality was bid as a relative haven within equities.
