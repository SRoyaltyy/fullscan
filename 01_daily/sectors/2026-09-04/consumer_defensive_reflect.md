CURRENT_BEHAVIOR: Model identifies NFP as a "two-sided binary" and scores S0_SHARED_MACRO=0, refusing to pre-score either tail. Despite identifying pre-existing duration stress (30Y 5.27, DFII10 rising, negative 10Y-SPX correlation), the model treats the hot and soft tails as equally likely/equally impactful, capping conviction at 0.5 and multiplier at 0.9.
CORRECTED_BEHAVIOR: When pre-existing duration stress is already identified (30Y in stress zone, real yields rising, strongly negative 10Y-SPX correlation), the model should recognize that the NFP binary is asymmetric for rate-sensitive sectors: a hot print reinforces an already-active hawkish channel (compounding pressure), while a soft print only relieves it. Score S0_SHARED_MACRO at −1 (not 0) for bond-proxy sectors when duration stress is pre-existing and the macro release could validate it. This does not mean pre-scoring the NFP outcome — it means recognizing the asymmetric sensitivity of the sector to the binary's hawkish tail given the existing regime.
