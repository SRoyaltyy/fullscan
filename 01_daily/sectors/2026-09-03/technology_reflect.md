CURRENT_BEHAVIOR: When a same-day two-sided macro catalyst (ISM Services) is identified, the model refuses to pre-score it (S0=0) and treats it as a reason to cap magnitude at flat/mild. When a live negative single-ticker catalyst (AVGO −3% to −6% AHR) is present, the model refuses to let it set the sector down (single-ticker veto) and also refuses to treat it as a dampener on the up path. The model treats "two-sided" as "no information" rather than "high variance with direction unresolved until the print." The model also treats the single-ticker veto symmetrically — it blocks AVGO from setting XLK down but does not recognize that a mega-cap-heavy sector with one large red name can still rally hard on a macro catalyst if the other mega-caps (NVDA/MSFT/AAPL) are green and the catalyst is risk-on.

CORRECTED_BEHAVIOR: When a same-day two-sided macro catalyst is identified AND the sector has a live single-ticker catalyst that is market-negative, the model should recognize that the two-sided catalyst creates elevated absolute-move risk in BOTH directions, and the flat band is the least likely outcome if the catalyst resolves with any conviction. Specifically: (1) A two-sided macro catalyst with a consensus forecast (~54.3 ISM) and a market that has sold off 3d (−1.89% rel) has asymmetric upside risk — a beat produces a short-covering rip in the most-crowded-short sector (semis #1 at 53% long but off from 82%, meaning recent sellers are underweight and will chase). (2) The single-ticker veto should apply to the DOWN path only when the negative name is not confirmed by breadth/leaders; it should NOT suppress the UP path when the macro catalyst is risk-on and the other mega-caps are green. (3) When S3=−1 (crowding) is the only negative and the macro catalyst is two-sided, the crowding factor should be treated as fuel for a short-covering rally on a beat, not as a persistent lid. (4) The model should assign a minimum magnitude band of mild (not flat) whenever a two-sided macro catalyst with consensus forecast is scheduled, because the flat band requires BOTH the catalyst to be a non-event AND the single-ticker negative to transmit — a conjunction that is less likely than either single path. (5) When the model's own Channel 2 text says "real two-sided event risk" and "do not write 'no macro print,' do not pre-score a miss," it should translate that into a wider predicted band (flat-to-up or flat-to-down with the direction conditional on the print), not a flat point estimate.
