- Nine of 11 S&P 500 ($SPX) sectors advanced on the day.
- The 10-year Treasury yield rose to 5.24% as the national debt exceeded $40.3 trillion and consumer sentiment slipped to its lowest level since May.
- After-hours trading was muted with no major earnings moves above 2%, and investors are looking ahead to big bank earnings next week from firms including JPMorgan Chase (JPM) and Goldman Sachs (GS).
