### STEP 2 — INTERACTIONS

- **Rising Yields + Risk-Off Tape + Gold Surge**: This is a classic "risk-off" cluster. The gold surge is a direct response to the same macro drivers (softer data, geopolitical risk) that are pushing yields higher and equities lower. Do not treat gold as a separate, independent bullish signal for all of XLB; it is a substitution trade out of risk assets.
- **Oil Spike + Higher Yields + Weak Equity Tape**: This is a stagflationary signal. It is a negative for cyclicals (XLI, XLY) and a positive for energy (XLE) and potentially defensive sectors. Do not buy cyclicals on dips without a clear reversal in oil or yields.
- **Crowded Tech + Risk-Off Tape**: The hedge fund positioning data suggests tech is a crowded long. In a risk-off tape with rising yields, this makes the sector more vulnerable to a sharper selloff. Do not buy tech on weakness without a clear catalyst.
