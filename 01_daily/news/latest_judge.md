### STEP 2 — INTERACTIONS

- **Fed path + weak labor**: The Fed Minutes (hawkish) and the "softer data" driving gold (dovish) create a conflicting macro signal. This is a **mixed** rates cluster. The hawkish Fed minutes are the more recent and direct signal for the Fed path, so they should dominate the rates channel. Do not double-count the "softer data" as a dovish offset for equities when the Fed is signaling a hike.
- **Yields up (buyback fizzle) + cyclicals/small-caps**: The failure of the buyback rally and the subsequent rise in yields is a **negative** for cyclicals and small-caps, which are more rate-sensitive. This does not support a risk-on breadth move.
- **AI chip demand + competitive dynamics**: The Marvell-Google deal is a **dispersion** event within the AI chip complex. It is not a uniform positive or negative for the sector. Do not treat it as a single directional signal for SMH.
- **Gold surge + risk-off tape**: The gold surge is a **flight-to-safety** trade, confirming the risk-off regime. It is a positive for gold miners but a negative for broad equities. This is a substitution effect, not a risk-on signal.
