## STEP 3 — TEST INTERACTIONS BETWEEN FACTORS

### AMPLIFICATION: Oil spike × Fed hike expectations × Yield backup
The single most important interaction of the day. The US-Iran tanker escalation pushed oil above $100, which feeds directly into inflation expectations, which raises the probability of a Warsh-led September rate hike, which pushes 10-year yields to multi-year highs. Each factor compounds the others: oil → inflation → Fed → yields → equity multiple compression. This is a **stagflation amplification loop** that the morning prediction correctly identified.

### OFFSETTING / MASKING: Treasury buyback announcement vs. 10-year auction demand
The Treasury's $6B buyback announcement initially pushed yields HIGHER (markets focused on supply/mechanics), but the strong 10-year auction at 1 PM provided offsetting relief. These two forces roughly cancelled in the afternoon, explaining why the SPX didn't cascade further after midday.

### CONDITIONAL / MODIFIER: Apple event as a sentiment modifier
The Apple event acted as a **modifier** on tech sentiment. While the broader tape was negative, Apple's event (and the stock's recovery from −1.4% to −0.28%) provided a modest cushion for the NDX, which closed at −0.64% vs the Dow's −0.77%. Without the Apple event, tech could have been weaker.

### SHARED-ROOT DOUBLE-COUNTING: The oil/geopolitical shock
The US-Iran tanker escalation is the **root cause** behind multiple "separate" factors: oil >$100, energy sector gains, industrials weakness, inflation expectations, Fed hike odds, yield backup, and even the Treasury buyback announcement (a policy response to yield pressure). These are symptoms of ONE cause, not independent factors. The morning prediction correctly scored this across B1 (catalyst), B7 (oil transmission), B2 (yields), B3 (Fed path), and B4 (VIX backwardation) — each capturing a distinct transmission channel.
