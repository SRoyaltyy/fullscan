# Upper rows → later rows (few-day path)

Success is not “tomorrow only.” Success is: **anything already
printed on a higher (older) row that reliably says the next 1–5
sessions are generally up or generally down**, with no time leak.

Same-row H/I are never features. Lag ≥ 1 of any letter is fair at
the next open. This pass does **not** use same-row fills (even the
timing-tested morning ones). Those are a different clock.

Labels (from the open after the feature is known): leftover H that
day, then buy-open / sell-close over ~2d, ~3d, ~1w. Long and short.

See `UPPER_LOWER.md` and `UPPER_STORIES.md`. Shorts are
`−return − fee`. Headline: CE (up) / CD (down) extremum pair;
JB / AH-red / HE / AM continuation downs. `CF` still dies.
