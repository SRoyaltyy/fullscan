How this is graded

  1. Liquid common stocks only: prior close ≥ $2 and prior dollar volume ≥ $5M. Indexes, inverse, and leveraged products skipped.
  2. Features come from bars with date < D, the prior Finviz export, or morning S. Same-day tape is the outcome.
  3. spy_cc_down answers “what won during a red market.” prior_spy_red is the tradable cousin.
  4. Excel columns A B C G J K L M O are start-of-day; D E F H I N use that day’s OHLC and must not gate a 09:30 pick.
  5. Finviz lifts use a tagged-only baseline so a missing export cannot inflate the hurdle.
