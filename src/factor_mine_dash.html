How a recipe is graded
  1. Leak-free 09:30 inputs only. Cameras, 🔵/🚨, E/R/D, prior news, 20-bar OHLC, and 8-bar candles must be knowable before the open. Same-day Change% / Gap / RelVol / printed book never pick. News uses the morning box or the prior Finviz export — never a later scrape or post-09:30 headline.
  2. Time. Buy at the 09:30 open. Hold H sessions (entry day counts). Optional early exit at a later 09:30 if 🚨, last-bar red, or news🔴 prints that morning.
  3. Win rate. Share of picks with horizon return > 0 (long or short).
  4. $ days / average win vs loss. Share of sessions whose cash book made money (leftover split, fees, whole shares), plus average closed-trade win % vs loss %.
  5. Sustainability. For every start date X, replay the cash book from X to the end. Count how often that path is still green. The cash-start scroller is that replay: $10k and no lots on morning X, same hard-red / leftover / min-hold rules. It is not “add on top of the 8-13 book.”
  6. Stock investigator. Dropdown = names that sleeve actually bought. Date scroller = 09:30 that morning. Cameras / coaches / news / tape are quoted from repo files — never same-day Change%.
  7. Top-25 gainer capture. Buy 8-17 hold=3 → did a pick land in the liquid top-25 gainers on 8-17, 8-18, or 8-19?
  8. Loser capture. Same window: names that printed Change% < −1.5% (outcome only — never a 09:30 gate).
  9. AvgW vs AvgL. Closed-trade mean % on the cash book (fees in).
  10. 09:30 open audit. Each session starts with cash (yesterday leftover, no overnight fees) and lots still held, re-marked at the open. vs yday close is the sum of those per-name overnight moves. Every held name also prints its 09:30 open, prior close, overnight $, close, and open→close $ — including days with zero fills. BUY does not label equity (cash↔stock). SELL/COVER labels the change after that sale: green if the sale locked in a gain, red if a loss.
  11. Butterfly state. Morning leftover cash + lots actually held are the only inputs. Size = leftover / rank_w / topheavy / half. Sell = list / time / cut_loser / trail. S≥+5 can size-up or add names, still cash-capped. Hard-red S≤−3 sits.
  12. Entry. flatten_h* buys the wish-list on io/HOLD mornings — live Action those days is HOLD. flatten_live_* only buys when the flatten gate fires (mover).
