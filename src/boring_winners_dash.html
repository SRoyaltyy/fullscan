How a sleeve is built

  1. source — book is the raw 1d BUY list. overlay keeps that list, drops fade, swaps stack-less names, adds gated extras. mine is the old 25-seat dump (comparison only).
  2. seats — 10 / 25 / 50. Thin BUY mornings stay thin unless extras can fill.
  3. hold N — a name bought on D cannot be sold until it has aged N sessions. Locked names keep their seat. Today's new buys only fill empties; if the book is full they are skipped.
  4. color — blue keeps 🔵 names only. green keeps panel cond=good.
  5. HARD_RED (lattice from 2026-08-31) — stand-down no new buys. limit −5% and −5% fill only fill at open×0.95 if that session's low printed through the limit. No print → limit skips; haircut fills at the 16:00 ET close. Close-to-close ≤ −5 is not enough.
  6. Futubull fees — same schedule as the paper blotter (00_grounding/futubull_fees.json). Whole shares only. New buys split leftover cash equally; skip if the split cannot buy 1 share after fees.
  7. orig 8/13–8/21 — same sleeve, cut to the window before the late-Aug drawdown / HARD_RED lattice. Start equity is the prior close (or $10k if the sleeve begins inside the window).
