## 1. Candidate A — `loud_tail_d8_v1`

| blank | value |
| --- | --- |
| `x_pct` | 10 |

`x_pct = 10` means the top 10 percent of that day's high-move half, ranked by `m1`, highest first. It is a tenth of the parent set, not 10 names and not the whole half.

`d1_hi` and `d1_lo` are percentiles of `d1` computed inside that loud set only. Higher `d1` is the long side. Long when the percentile is above 0.90. Short when the percentile is at or below 0.10. A loud name between those tails is not held.

Ties on a cutoff stay in. Percentiles use average ranks. Weights stay the preregistration rule: equal weight within each side, longs sum to +1, shorts sum to −1, an empty side sums to 0. There is no short-share target.

These are round numbers, stricter than the peeked loudest ~20%. The roughly four live D8 days behind that 79% / +2.2% note are burned. They are discovery only. They were not used to pick 10, 0.90, or 0.10, and they do not enter this ledger.
