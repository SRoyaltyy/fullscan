What the columns mean

  1. ideal — official 09:30 open. Optimistic published column, not best-possible.
  2. limit_prior — working limit at prior close. Fill iff session trades through (buy: low < limit; short: high > limit). Miss = $0 position, leftover stays.
  3. limit_open — same trade-through at the official open.
  4. market_mid — PR 241 delayed market: 35% of post-open adverse room, α from prior sessions only.
  5. market_adverse — buy at high / short at low when that print exists. WORST plausible.
  6. market_favorable — buy at low / short at high. BEST plausible, labeled unlikely.
  7. partial_50 — 50% of market_mid shares (floor 1 if intended ≥ 2; 1-share all-or-none).
  8. gap_miss — |open−prior|/prior ≥ 8% against the order → miss.

Daily OHLC has no true tape. Limit trade-through is low < limit (buy) / high > limit (short). Market path walks the printed high/low room. A missing official open is a miss — close is never substituted.
