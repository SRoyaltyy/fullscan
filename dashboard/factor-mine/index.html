Start-date sustainability (would it still make money?)

If the recipe starts at the 09:30 open of day X, how did the $10k book do through the last finished close? A session that has already closed (e.g. 9/9 while 9/10 is still pre-open) prints a preliminary open\u2192close %. An unopened morning is pending — not 0%.

How a recipe is graded
  1. Leak-free 09:30 inputs only. Cameras, 🔵/🚨, E/R/D, prior news, 20-bar OHLC, 60-bar RSI/MACD, and 8-bar candles must be knowable before the open. Same-day Change% / Gap / RelVol / printed book never pick. News uses the morning box, the session-morning Finviz export when News Time is before 09:30, or the prior export — never a later scrape or post-09:30 headline. RSI prefers the prior Finviz Elite print; MACD is computed on prior bars. Flow-in is prior rel-vol ≥ 1.5 while |1-day %| ≤ 1.2 — money arrived, price barely moved.
  2. Time. Buy at the 09:30 open. Hold H sessions (entry day counts). Optional early exit at a later 09:30 if 🚨, last-bar red, or news🔴 prints that morning.
  3. Win rate. Share of picks with horizon return > 0 (long or short).
  4. $ days / average win vs loss. Share of sessions whose cash book made money (leftover split, fees, whole shares), plus average closed-trade win % vs loss %.
  5. Sustainability. For every start date X, replay the cash book from X's 09:30 open through the last session that has a 16:00 close. Count how often that path is still green. A start on 9/9 while 9/10 has not opened is the 9/9 open\u2192close book, labelled prelim. An unopened start date is pending, not 0%. Cash-start << < > >> is that replay: $10k and no lots on morning X, same hard-red / leftover / min-hold rules. It is not “add on top of the 8-13 book.”
  6. Stock investigator. Dropdown = names that sleeve actually bought. Date buttons = 09:30 that morning. A big BUY / SIT / NO badge says whether this recipe would take the name, plus the gates that fired or failed. E / R dots are beat-miss / upgrade-downgrade from the session-morning Finviz EPS Surprise when the print is already public (yday AMC / today BMO). A date-only stamp with no surprise number stays unknown — not green. Today's AMC is not used. Horizon % is hold-H from the 09:30 open. Cameras / coaches / news are quoted from repo files — never same-day Change%.
  7. Morning board. Every name this recipe looked at that 09:30, ranked by its own rank key, plus would-be horizon % if bought here. Side is the sleeve's intended trade (LONG or SHORT). Buy is the 09:30 open (the fill). Sell / cover is the exit print — horizon close, or a later 09:30 open if 🚨 / last-red / news🔴 fired, or if sell% take/stop is on and that 09:30 open hit the bracket. Sell is green when that exit locked a profit for the sleeve's side (long: sell > buy; short: cover < buy), else red. E is the leak-free morning-export EPS surprise (beat / miss / unknown) — same number as the investigator chip. Cams is +green −red camera counts (e.g. +5 −1). Yday is yesterday's close-to-close % — never today's tape. RSI / MACD / Flow are prior-session only: RSI OS/OB (≤30 / ≥70), MACD hist + or X+ (just crossed up), FLOW IN when volume arrived and price barely moved. Hit-rate tally covers BUY / LOOK / NO / SIT, polarity hits vs the actual price move, and the ≤2 vs ≥3 red-camera split. The white+yday toggle keeps this looker list but buys only 0-red names that were up yesterday, ranked by +G−R. sell% in the filter bar replays every single recipe with a 09:30 take and/or stop (open vs our fill, even inside min-hold). Cards, table Book%, and curves switch to the replay; each Book% shows was / Δ vs the baked hold. Combos stay on the baked shared-pile book. The morning-board take/stop box is the same control. Marks chips, blotter BUY rows, and the session bought list also print that E so a beat like INO +67.7% is visible without opening the investigator.
  8. Open-bell slip / no-fill. Research overlay only. Same 09:30 shopping lists, three fill realities: ideal official open, MARKET-like delay + adverse slip (α from prior OHLC, this session’s high/low is the path after the order), LIMIT at the intended open or prior close (fill or miss). KEEP bar ≥30 filled fires and >55% after-fee hit rate vs the zero-slip baseline. Does not change live flatten_robust, hard-red sit, or Webull paper.
  9. −N / burst / green-news sweep. Research overlays on the current cash-book leaders. A new gate is kept only if it beats flatten_h5 on the $10k book. If none do, current methods stay. Live flatten_robust is never changed.
  10. News packet / headline / cameras. Green is OR for the looker: packet🟢 or headline🟢 — both are not required, and +9 −1 was only an example. On 08-13→09-11 $10k the OR set itself does not beat flatten_h5 (union_news_or_h1 +4.98); adding a camera-net floor to that OR set also lost. What printed is packet-green hold-1 with a mild positive net: union_news_pack_net2_h1 (+G −R ≥ 2) +16.53. Packet with no net floor is +12.49. +9 −1 all-in and leftover conviction still lose. A gate stays only if the $10k book beats flatten_h5.
  11. RSI / MACD / flow-in. Oversold (RSI≤30), overbought (RSI≥70), MACD histogram > 0 or a fresh cross up, and flow-in (volume without a matching price move) are 09:30 gates on the same looker list. Shorts use overbought / MACD down. Rankers rsi (more oversold first) and macd_hist sit next to the existing hot / cond ranks. Same leak rule: prior Finviz export + prior bars only.
  12. Top-25 gainer capture. Buy 8-17 hold=3 → did a pick land in the liquid top-25 gainers on 8-17, 8-18, or 8-19?
  13. Loser capture. Same window: names that printed Change% < −1.5% (outcome only — never a 09:30 gate).
  14. AvgW vs AvgL. Closed-trade mean % on the cash book (fees in).
  15. 09:30 open audit. Each session starts with cash (yesterday leftover, no overnight fees) and lots still held, re-marked at the open. vs yday close is the sum of those per-name overnight moves. Every held name also prints its 09:30 open, prior close, overnight $, close, and open→close $ — including days with zero fills. BUY does not label equity (cash↔stock). SELL/COVER labels the change after that sale: green if the sale locked in a gain, red if a loss.
  16. Butterfly state. Morning leftover cash + lots actually held are the only inputs. Size = leftover / rank_w / topheavy / half. Sell = list / time / cut_loser / trail. S≥+5 can size-up or add names, still cash-capped. Hard-red S≤−3 sits.
  17. Entry. flatten_h* buys the wish-list on io/HOLD mornings — live Action those days is HOLD. flatten_live_* only buys when the flatten gate fires (mover).
  18. Combinations. A combo_* row is the same $10k cash book as every other recipe — leftover split, whole shares, Futubull fees, sell first, 09:30 open, hard-red S≤−3 sit. Same table columns, same leftover · list · none line, same three-column explain. It is not a mashed shopping list and not a blend of daily return series. At 09:30 each member still runs its own leak-free pick_day (same cameras / prior news / prior tape; never today’s Change%). Shared pile: one leftover-cash stack; unused cash spills in claim order (fresh-E, then heat, then other longs, then shorts); weights cap each kid’s slice of whatever cash is left. That spill is why a shared mix can beat both kids — it is not the average of their Book%. A short fill adds cash (borrow) that can later fund a long, still capped by equity ≥ 2× notional. Split pile: each member is a normal book at its weight × $10k, then the books are added (two accounts; the same name can sit in both). One ticker, one side on a shared pile. Each lot keeps the owner’s min-hold and list-drop — a hold-3 fresh-E lot is not dumped by a hold-1 heat card. Lots already held are not sold just because S is red. WIN (next to the name, same place as thin) means this cash Book% is higher than every member, or it stays within 2pp of the richest member with a smaller drawdown than every member, the best start-rate, both halves green, and a no-worse worst day. Signal% / Eff do not award WIN. Eighteen sessions rank this tape. Live flatten_robust is unchanged.
  19. Long-led. We do not only win via shorts. Closed-trade $ is stamped long vs short on every sleeve. Long-led means long $ ≥ short $, Book% ≥ 20, and start YES ≥ 75% (16/21). The +35 combo_sh_5050 is ~47% long / 53% short — the long is union_hot_n4_h1, which made most of its money in one week (8/21–8/25) and is red if you start after that burst (10/21 starts). The short is the grind that keeps the combo’s start chips green. If you want longs to carry: union_e_fresh_h3 (pure long +23, 16/21), combo_ej_5050 (two longs, no short), combo_jse_333 (~80% long $), combo_es_9010 (90% fresh-E / 10% short). The Long-led filter is that bar.
