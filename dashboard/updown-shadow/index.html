updown_rel1_hv_v1 — frozen pre-open picks and running realized record

PAPER ONLY — failed multiple-testing haircut (t 2.02 vs 3.37). Shadow record, no orders, no broker wiring.
What this is
  • Every trading day, before 09:25 ET (21:25 HKT), a frozen LightGBM model ranks the top 1,000 US names by 20-day dollar volume (actual prior close ≥ $5). It uses only data knowable before the 09:30 open, with no same-day open.
  • It keeps the half with the largest predicted 1-day move. It goes long the top 10% and short the bottom 10% of a 1-day sector-relative ranker within that half (about 50 / 50, equal weight).
  • Fill: the 09:30 official open, with exit at the next session's 09:30 open (Yahoo split-adjusted). Rebalanced daily. Fees = bp/side × turnover.
  • Pick files are committed and fingerprinted before 09:25 ET and never edited. Results are computed after the close into a separate ledger.
  • Round-3 research: confirm-window t 2.31, but cumulative t 2.02 is below the 3.37 bar, so it stays paper-only. Rules and kill/promote criteria: research/updown_rel1_hv_v1.
  • Related: dashboard/updown, which carries the expected-move rank column (updown_expmove_v1).
