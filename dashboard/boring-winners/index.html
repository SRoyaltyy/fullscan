How a sleeve is built

  1. source — book is the raw 1d BUY list. overlay keeps that list, drops fade, swaps stack-less names, adds gated extras. mine is the old 25-seat dump (comparison only).
  2. seats — 10 / 25 / 50. Thin BUY mornings stay thin unless extras can fill.
  3. hold N — a name bought on D cannot be sold until it has aged N sessions. Locked names keep their seat. Today's new buys only fill empties; if the book is full they are skipped.
  4. color — blue keeps 🔵 names only. green keeps panel cond=good.
  5. HARD_RED (lattice from 2026-08-31) — stand-down no new buys. −5% fill assumes we got 5% cheaper on new buys that morning. limit −5% only buys if that name's 1d is ≤ −5.
