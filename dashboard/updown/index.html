Every-stock morning up/down read — updown_o2c_v2

RESEARCH ONLY — NOT A TRADING SIGNAL.
  • The up-probabilities use each day's 09:30 official open, and that same open is the entry price of the open→close result. You cannot know the official open before an opening order is committed, so the edge shown cannot be traded as printed.
  • A version that knows only the prior close (no same-day open) scores AUC about 0.52 with no after-fee edge (dev long-short −0.20%/day after 0.4% fees; holdout +0.07%/day, not significant).
  • The data carries survivorship bias: the stock list holds only companies still trading in 2026, so companies that died in 2018–2025 are missing.
  • Full leak audit: LEAK_AUDIT.md.
  • Exp. move rank column (updown_expmove_v1) is a descriptive risk rank, not a trading signal. It shows the predicted size of the move from today's open to the next open, |open→next open|, ranked within the top 1,000 names by dollar volume (100 = biggest expected move). It uses only pre-open inputs. It beats plain 20-day Parkinson volatility only slightly (rank IC about 0.42 vs 0.38). Past days are walk-forward out-of-sample; from 2026-10-07, days are frozen before 09:25 ET (see the card below). Paper shadow strategy: updown-shadow.
