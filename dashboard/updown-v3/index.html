Is it better than guessing? (all tickers, walk-forward)

Brier score = average of (chance − outcome)², lower is better. A coin flip (always 50%) scores 0.2500. "Base rate" always guesses the share of ups seen in the training years. A horizon gets the green badge only if it beats both with t > 2 on non-overlapping days.
Reliability: predicted chance (x) vs how often it actually went up (y), per horizon. On the diagonal = honest.
